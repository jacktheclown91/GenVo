**Purpose:** Collate approved and documented future development without re-listing already implemented foundations as unfinished. This is a planning inventory, not a new implementation or creator approval.

## Status key
- **PARTIAL:** A real code system/vertical slice exists, but the intended expansion is unfinished.
- **NOT IMPLEMENTED:** Planned system or workflow has no completed general implementation in the inspected v1.73 baseline.
- **QUALITY / ACCEPTANCE:** Functionality exists, but visual, usability, balancing, test or deployment acceptance remains open.
- **LATER / OPTIONAL:** Recorded longer-range option, not an immediate promised feature.

## A. Emergent species, genetics, and long-term evolution
1. **PARTIAL — Remove fixed species-ID biological authority.** Current founding taxa and base metadata still influence mate eligibility, life histories, spawn populations and select social/ecological assumptions. Let actual anatomy, genetics, physiology, habitat and inherited behavior determine capabilities. Preserve founding IDs only where needed for old saves and ancestry; avoid creating a second biology engine.
2. **PARTIAL — Genuine inherited reproductive compatibility and reproductive isolation.** Compatibility should evolve through hereditary/developmental divergence, gene flow, ecological specialization and mating preferences; related branches can sometimes hybridize while isolated populations may become reproductively incompatible. Replace simplistic same-species gates without bypassing physical viability/kinship.
3. **PARTIAL — Species formation recognized from actual population divergence.** The current speciation tree and vocabulary name generator exist, but broad autonomous wild speciation and newly distinct reproductive groups need extension; do not equate a generated name with a new species or hardcoded behavior.
4. **PARTIAL — Wildlife co-evolution across all taxa.** Wild offspring already inherit body plans; scale beyond founder templates toward independently evolving anatomies, distributions, diets, niches, predators and reproductive strategies. Support legitimate extinction, migration and hybridization without outcome guarantees.
5. **PARTIAL — Full genetic/developmental variation.** Expand expression/proportion ranges, precursors/intermediate adaptations, ecological selection, costs and divergent inherited strategies. Preserve the rule that new body designs affect future offspring, not living adults.
6. **PARTIAL — Selective breeding and stable-body-plan editor feedback.** Current matching-build editor gate and crossed-body offspring are implemented; improve discoverable partner comparisons, breeding-line tracking, practical 2–3-generation stabilization pathways, and clarity about uncertain inheritance (no fixed outcome guarantees).
7. **LATER / OPTIONAL — Detailed speciation/selection visualizations.** Provide lineage phylogenies, population-level trait histories, compatibility/gene-flow evidence and recorded niche transitions, grounded in actual world history.

## B. Mating, courtship, social systems, reproductive evolution
8. **PARTIAL — Richer anatomy-enabled courtship performances.** Evolve from the present five action categories and short 3-action queues to real eight-frame dances, variable timing, interruption, calls, reciprocation, responsive gestures, and distinctive anatomy-driven animations/audio.
9. **PARTIAL — Cultural ritual learning.** Learn from failure as well as success, observations, cross-lineage demonstrations, environmental context, teaching, innovations and generational drift. Rituals remain optional advantages, never forced pregnancy prerequisites.
10. **PARTIAL — Social and mating competition.** Contextual rivals, risk evaluation, dominance displays, territory, retreats, physical contests, alliances, social reputation, cooperation and selection pressure reporting. No universal sex roles, unbeatable winners or guaranteed acceptance.
11. **PARTIAL — Explainable mate choice and discovery.** Show why two real animals accept/refuse (preferences, condition, genetic/structural compatibility, kinship, maturity, environment); provide real tracks, calls, scent trails and witnessed activity instead of omniscient later-generation mate markers.
12. **PARTIAL — More sophisticated reproductive anatomy.** Egg chamber, live womb and dual mode already work at a basic level. Future: adaptive inherited reproductive tactics, variable clutch/investment decisions, richer parent–young tradeoffs and detailed reproductive animations, without making dual capability automatically superior.
13. **PARTIAL — Expanded eggs/nesting/parenting.** Nest variety and material choices, guarding, stealing, carrying eggs, incubation tradeoffs, specialized egg predators, better egg/nest art and audio, and meaningful care strategies. Existing physical eggs, limited care, paid nests and egg predation are foundations.

## C. Player gameplay between generations
14. **PARTIAL — More meaningful body-gated action verbs.** Existing Dig, Pounce, strike, venom bite, brace and basic part actions are a start. Historical proposals include Ram (armor), Swim-dash (suitable swimming anatomy), Display/intimidation/rally, toxin spit (appropriate glands/organ), and deeper climbing, grappling, stealth, defense and gathering actions only when supported by anatomy.
15. **PARTIAL — Encounter diversity.** Predator–predator competition, rival foraging, contested food, active hunts, territorial disputes, scavenging and survival choices must be genuine simulated interactions rather than scripted showcases.
16. **PARTIAL — Engaging activities while waiting for offspring.** More discovery, survival decisions, social play, shelter work, quests and consequences in the first 5–20 minutes; player should not be sitting through a reproductive timer.
17. **PARTIAL — Family behavior.** Better child following across diverse movement modes, flee/retreat learning, cooperative warning/defense, offspring teaching, kinship, parental roles, mature family settlement and family home/shelter choices.
18. **PARTIAL — Long-term self-directed lineage objectives.** Hold territory, encounter rival clans, explore/settle frontier habitats, establish multi-generational nests/colonies, and make different lineages worth playing; no compulsory rigid quest chain.
19. **PARTIAL — Generation 8 legacy milestone.** Meaningful introductory lineage review of ancestors, traits, survival, discoveries, ecological effects and choices, with indefinite generations beyond the eighth.
20. **QUALITY / ACCEPTANCE — First-session usability.** Replace opaque task walls with contextual learning, explain action failures and survival tradeoffs, test actual first-time play, and preserve the rule that gameplay teaches the game.

## D. Guided Survival and alternate play modes
21. **NOT IMPLEMENTED (full system) — Default Guided Survival profile.** Introduce explicit, persisted world-mode rules and a clear world creation explanation; preserve existing legacy saves and avoid implying future modes work.
22. **PARTIAL — Deterministic start validator.** Jointly validate accessible travel, food, water, climate, starter mate, viable escape route and visible ecology for the founder's actual anatomy; transparently reject invalid manually specified seeds. Current starter placement is simpler.
23. **PARTIAL — Bounded transparent onboarding protection.** Founder and designated starter mate only, explicit expiry and on-screen notification; no later reactivation, ecology tampering, forced successful reproduction or invisible rescue mates.
24. **PARTIAL — Fair but dangerous first minutes.** Actual observed predators, competing grazers, rival interactions and survival signals; meaningful danger without arbitrarily impossible starts.
25. **LATER / OPTIONAL — Sandbox/Creator mode.** Relax mutation budget restrictions for experimentation while retaining body grammar, validity and ecological rules; separate sandbox achievements from survival history.
26. **LATER / OPTIONAL — Primordial/Cell origin.** Within the same persistent world model, playable microscopic aquatic lineages with cilia/flagella, feeding, oxygen, growth and a potential (not mandatory) path to larger bodies or continued aquatic specialization.

## E. Shared world: freshwater, underground, regions, and biomes
27. **PARTIAL — Richer aquatic ecology.** Freshwater resource and feeding systems already exist. Extend viable underwater producer–consumer chains, oxygen/light/temperature, shelter, breeding, larvae/fry, seasonal productivity, crossings, amphibious predation, water-limited anatomy and actual water-only lineages.
28. **NOT IMPLEMENTED (connected underground habitat) — Sparse tunnel network.** Persist actual entrances, chambers and interconnected passages; require anatomical digging and passability; track rock, soil hardness, temperature, moisture, roots, fungi, grubs, oxygen and tunnels that can flood/collapse.
29. **PARTIAL — Regional ecological distinctness.** The current region controller observes chunks, births/deaths and stock; it is not a climate/biome or unloaded-population simulation. Build condition-based habitat zones and pressure differences across surface/freshwater/underground terrain.
30. **PARTIAL — Seasonal ecosystems.** Actual region-specific temperature, moisture, daylight, plant growth/dormancy, winter food access and aquatic conditions, beyond existing global season/day cycles.
31. **NOT IMPLEMENTED (deep strategies) — Overwintering and seasonal adaptation.** Torpor/hibernation with reserves and dangers, food caches that can spoil/be stolen, cold physiological adaptation, realistic winter migration and insulated burrow living.
32. **PARTIAL — Varied plants and food webs.** More finite biome-specific grasses, flowers, bushes, roots/tubers, fungi, underwater plants/algae, larvae, carcass recycling, seed banks and condition-based germination. New content must have gameplay/ecological functionality, not simply decoration.
33. **PARTIAL — Distant regional simulation.** Introduce near-field fully simulated organisms and far-field genotype/phenotype cohort aggregates, with continuous migrations and deterministic reconciliation; current region records are observation-only.

## F. Living Planet: persistent environmental change
34. **PARTIAL — First biology-to-habitat feedback.** Digging creates soil disturbance and some animal actions transport seeds. Expand accountable habitat engineering: grazing, disturbance, seed dispersal, plant establishment, bank excavation, substrate change and silt effects. Work must be performed and consume finite resources.
35. **NOT IMPLEMENTED — Unified persistent world/environment authority.** Versioned region material/soil/water state and slow ecological/environmental update clocks, real causal write batches and finite transfers. Biomes are condition readings, not arbitrary replacement maps.
36. **NOT IMPLEMENTED — Hydrological change.** Runoff, drainage, connected water stores, floods, sediment transport, groundwater, waterway and shoreline shifts that change real routes, nests and ecosystem productivity.
37. **NOT IMPLEMENTED — Slow erosion, deposition, geomorphology.** Terrain material transport, erosion, changing channels, banks, coasts and eventual topological changes with navigation invalidation and save fidelity.
38. **NOT IMPLEMENTED — Geological activity.** Faults, uplift, volcano material sources and major terrain changes on appropriate timescales; no automatic 100-generation geology schedule.
39. **NOT IMPLEMENTED — Sudden natural events.** Environmental prerequisites → observable warning → spatial damage/flow → persistent recovery for local storms/tornadoes, floods, droughts, heat, blizzards, fires, eventual hurricanes (requires oceans), eruptions and very rare meteor impacts. Existing weather/exposure is not this event engine.
40. **NOT IMPLEMENTED — Long-term environmental history viewer.** Region keyframes, former shorelines/plant cover, extinctions/recolonizations, creature engineering receipts, time comparisons and actual evidence of landscape change.
41. **LATER / OPTIONAL — Geological epoch/extended-time operation.** Fast-forward slow processes only while ecology, reproduction and ancestry are integrated credibly; checkpoint/resume/cancellation and approximation disclosure are mandatory.

## Boundaries and superseded proposals
- **GenVo** is the public name; old GenVo8 identifiers can remain for compatibility.
- **32×32 is the chosen native art direction.** Additional detail requires better pixels/animation/scale handling, not an unapproved resolution change.
- **Rule of 8 is a design convention, not a content maximum or mandatory end after Generation 8.**
- **Hardcoded new species lists are not the expansion strategy.** Starting populations are permitted, but species identities and capacities must emerge from biology, ecology and gene flow.
- **Same-build mating offers next-generation editing; mixed related breeding recombines real parental parts.** Don't restore unrestricted editing after every conception.
- **Rituals are learned and optional; mate preferences are heritable, current condition is not a mate-preference gene.**
- **No manufactured ecological outcomes.** Population collapse, ecological success, extinction and failed reproduction can be legitimate outcomes.
- Older Oct 8 "evolution points at every birth" and rigid territory/era goal examples are **historical proposals**, not approvals to override the newer reproductive-editor and biological-selection rules.

## Suggested development sequencing (not an additional creator decision)
1. Complete **creature motion/readability and art-tool visual acceptance** on the v1.73 foundation.
2. Make **reproductive compatibility genuinely inherited and species emergence independent of founding IDs**; improve in-game lineage feedback.
3. Build a complete **body-enabled gameplay/social and mate-selection slice** that gives players meaningful activity between generations.
4. Establish **Guided Survival world generation and robust save/recovery** so onboarding and testing reflect real systems.
5. Extend **freshwater, connected underground habitats and regional seasons**, with functional plants and viable ecological alternatives.
6. Build **persistent world state and ecosystem engineering**, followed by weather/hydrology events, erosion/geology and history.
7. Consider **Cell origin, Sandbox and epoch simulation** after the shared model supports them.
