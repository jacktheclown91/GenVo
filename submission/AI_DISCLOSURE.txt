GenVo: AI content disclosure (Game Gauntlet SIM Jam, October 2026)

GenVo is a developer-directed original game by SevenFoldStories. The game's
concept, design, direction, selection of results, integration, testing and
final decisions are human-led. AI tools were used throughout production, as
follows.

Does the game use AI-generated content? Yes.

Code
  Most of the program code was written with AI coding assistants (Anthropic
  Claude via Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and ChatGPT) working under the
  developer's direction and review.

Art
  The creature and environment pixel art was produced with AI assistance. Some
  part and habitat artwork began as masters made with an AI image-generation
  tool, which were then converted, redrawn and assembled into the game's 32-pixel
  sprites with project tools. The rest is drawn by code the assistants wrote.
  Screenshots and the page cover are composed from the running game's own art.

Music and sound
  All music and sound effects are synthesized by the game's own code
  (tools/generate_audio_assets.py and the in-browser audio engine), which was
  written with AI assistance. No recordings, sample libraries or generative
  audio or music models were used.

Text
  In-game text, the help and tutorial copy, and this page's description were
  written with AI assistance and edited under the developer's direction.

Third-party assets
  None. The game uses no purchased or licensed asset packs, libraries or fonts
  (it uses the browser's system fonts).

Rights
  The developer confirms their rights to all content, and that it was made
  during the jam period, in the jam's signed submission attestation.
