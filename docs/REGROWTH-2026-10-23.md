# Regrowth from grazed ground (October 23, 2026)

**Creator request:** measure regrowth from grazed ground first (perennial regrowth from roots and seed rain, from a physical basis), as its own step on matched seeds.

## What holds the grazed belt bare

Under own-body reserves ([previous step](BODY-RESERVES-2026-10-23.md)), the belt within 6 tiles of water was 64% bare by day 151.

**Correction to that report:** plant growth is not logistic. The growth term, `growth × moisture × temperature × fertility × (1 − v/carry)`, does not depend on the plant already present.

**The real block is the plant's stored energy:**
- Growth is funded from the plant's stored reserve (`plant-resources.js`).
- Photosynthetic income is proportional to the standing biomass.
- Removing tissue (grazing, fire, dieback) removed the reserve in the same proportion.
- So a tile grazed to nothing kept no reserve and earned no income, and it never regrew.

**Measured at day 120 within 6 tiles of water:**
- bare tiles hold a reserve of 0.0011, against 0.153 on covered tiles;
- growth on bare tiles is limited by stored energy on 632 of 783 and by nutrients on 151;
- water and moisture fit are the same on both.

## What was built: `perennial-regrowth/1`, opt-in

`src/ecology/perennial-regrowth.js` is saved with each world and copied on crossings.

- **Root reserve.** Perennials hold most of their reserves below grazing height, in roots, rhizomes and crowns. Removing standing tissue now takes only the above-ground 40% of the reserve, and 60% stays (`rootShare`). Regrowth is funded from what remains, through the ordinary funded growth.
- **Seed and spore rain.** A tile with neither plant nor reserve is recolonised from its neighbours.
  - Each day it receives a propagule of 0.2% of the neighbours' mean standing biomass.
  - The propagule is taken from the neighbours' own biomass and reserve, so nothing is created.
  - Seed-limited plants keep their existing seed banks.
- **Measurement switches** (development only): `sim.correctionExperiment.rootReserve=false` and `.seedRain=false`.

**Test:** `tests/perennial-regrowth.mjs` (gate) checks that:
- grazing a tile to nothing keeps 60% of its reserve, while recorded worlds keep none;
- the grazed tile regrows within three days, while on recorded worlds it stays bare;
- seed rain conserves biomass and reserve;
- saves keep the policy.

## What it shows

- **On GENVO8-TEST-A regrowth helps, mainly through the root reserve.** Starvation more than halves (263 to 135, and 98 with both parts), and more animals are alive at the end of the year (30 against 4). But the population still declines through the year.
- **On PLANET-OBS-2 it makes no clear difference.**
- **On PLANET-OBS-3 a wildfire, not grazing, collapses the plants in both arms.**
  - Between days 63 and 78, fire burns about 17,000 of 20,000 units of plant biomass, from 14,327 ignitions.
  - On the recorded 6-second day the same seed's fire season burns 6,048 by day 90, and 20,200 of plants remain. On the 30-second day it burns 21,759, and 2,762 remain (`tools/probe-wildfire-clock.mjs`, [output](regrowth-2026-10-23/wildfire-PLANET-OBS-3-both-clocks.txt)).
  - The other two seeds have no fire in 90 days on either clock.
  - **Fire runs per tick, so the 30-second day multiplies a fire season about 3.6 times.** This is a day-clock defect of the same kind as the herd moves, and the next to fix.

## Decision

Perennial regrowth corrects a real flaw: grazed ground could never regrow. It reduces starvation where grazing is the binding limit, but it does not by itself make a year hold. It stays opt-in alongside the other v1 rules.

**Next candidates:**
1. **Wildfire per day rather than per tick on 30-second days:** a clear defect, measured.
2. **Breeding and founding estimates on real needs:** they still assume about a fifth of real needs, and births run ahead of what the land carries.
3. **Rillsifter food:** their algae are eaten out within the first month.
