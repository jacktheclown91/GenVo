# E6: dryland anatomical evolution

Content-first expansion stacked on E5's native top-down art, climate and inherited anatomy. **This is not a performance-only PR.**

## Three new biologically functional, inheritable parts

| Organ | Required ancestral precursor | Mutation cost | Real effect | Cost/tradeoff |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| **Heat frill** (dorsal) | Signal crest | 5 | 28% lower heat-exposure energy multiplier when ambient temperature is at or above the animal's comfort temperature | 14% greater cold-exposure factor, 0.22 part upkeep, 0.45 load |
| **Wax plates** (flank) | Armor plate | 4 | Up to 24% lower basal hydration deduction at the driest soil moisture, smoothly vanishing at 0.48 moisture | 18% greater hot-exposure factor, 0.18 part upkeep, 0.44 load |
| **Arid pad** (limb) | Padded leg | 3 | With at least two installed: up to 18% land-speed increase and 12% lower land movement cost at driest soil | On saturated soil, up to 10% slower and 14% higher movement cost; 0.16 upkeep and 0.46 load per foot |

Effects use the **actual canonical creature-mounted anatomy** and actual cell moisture/temperature. They apply through the same existing player and wildlife movement/metabolism authorities, not species labels or a biome-specific survival buff. No new material, water, energy, respiration, flight, or RNG sources exist. The inherited organs occupy existing physical sockets and require real precursor lineages; editing an adult does not grant them.

Heat frills can be combined with earlier filament/fur routes if an actual torso has space, but insulation and heat penalties still apply. Wax does not replace water access and cannot create water. The broad foot is not a new pathfinding or sand-layer implementation, only a supported terrain-contact adaptation.

The renderer has unique **top-down procedural review fallbacks**: radiator fin, flank plates and broad articulated toes. Full native edited 32x32, 8-direction art remains a separate asset task. No claim of finalized art or creator review.

## Development boundary

This is a targeted content pass with one focused test, not a full regression/performance run. `tests/e6-dryland-evolution.mjs` checks registry and help text, precursor mutation gating, valid mounts, physical hot/cold and dry/wet tradeoffs, actual shared movement, and actual metabolism hydration deductions. The lightweight workflow checks source syntax and runs only that test.

**Still missing:** independent continents and unloadable 3D regions, aerial flight, full tree/canopy layers, true mammalian physiology, more species generated by natural evolutionary branching, human-reviewed native part art, broad ecological balance. These require separate content milestones and later full regression verification.

This branch is stacked on `feat/e5-readable-cover-silhouettes`. Neither the P1-P3 performance experiments nor `main` were changed.
