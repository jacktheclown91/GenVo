# Local lineage appearance assortment | 2026-10-21

This is a narrow, policy-versioned first pass toward recognizable local families. `src/reproduction/mate-preferences.js` remains the single reciprocal choice authority. The `local-visual-assortment/1` rule is recorded for new worlds. Ordinary historical Continue with no rule keeps the previous symmetric founder similarity distribution, species-color fallback, old appearance weights and distance cost. Archived planetary regions must agree with the active policy.

For new worlds, founders draw similarity from -0.18 to +0.70 (mean +0.26) on their dedicated deterministic RNG stream, still retaining contrast-preferring individuals. Modern appearance scoring uses the exact inherited pigment hue rendered by the body palette, plus visible root torso, mounted leg types and other non-ocular organs. Distance slightly strengthens ordinal local choice. Reciprocal consent, physical reproductive compatibility, hybrid possibility, all reproduction resource gates and both sexes' choices remain independent.

**Not done:** lowered mutation chance, invented local-cohort gate or hard same-species preference. Natural 30–60 day family grouping, biodiversity and inbreeding outcomes still require measured independent trials, and first-generation player experience needs real Chromium/creator play. A soft preference cannot guarantee cohorts without compatible mates.

Focused contract: `tests/local-lineage-assortment.mjs`. The PR's workflow also runs existing reciprocal/inherited-choice, competition, courtship, save and source/export tests. Measured outcomes should distinguish controlled comparison from genuine emergent ecology.
