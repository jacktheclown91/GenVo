# GenVo 6-Month Systems Technical Roadmap

**Horizon:** November 2026 – May 2027  
**Vision:** Scale GenVo from an evolutionary survival prototype into an expansive, RimWorld-scale emergent ecological, social, and generational storytelling simulation.  
**Lead Systems Developer:** Antigravity  
**Presentation & UI Collaborator:** Codex  

---

## Strategic Goals

1. **Uncompromised Biological & Physical Grounding:** All emergent narratives must emerge from real simulated variables (thermoregulation, digestive anatomy, sensory acuity, social bonds, physical calories, subterranean mechanics).
2. **Deep Generational Continuity:** Lineages must remember ancestry, family traditions, learned behaviours, territorial conflicts, and ancestral graves across dozens of generations.
3. **Planetary Ecological Scale:** Seamless transitions across continental biomes, deep cave systems, and marine food webs without loading screens, pop-in, or memory bloat.

---

## Phase 1 (Months 1–2): Subterranean Ecology, Deep Speciation & Macro-Predation

### Milestones & Deliverables
- **M1.1: Deep Karst & Subsurface Cavern Networks (Completing Issue #34)**
  - Expand fracture-chambers into continuous multi-stratum cave systems extending 3–5 layers beneath the surface.
  - Implement real subterranean microclimates: geothermal vents, stagnant CO2 sinks, and underground river aquifers feeding surface springs.
  - Subterranean troglobite adaptations: complete pigment loss, blindness paired with echolocation crests, and metabolic stasis.
- **M1.2: Macro-Predation & Apex Pack Tactics**
  - Group coordination algorithms: cooperative flanking, ambush rings around watering holes, and pack hunting roles (chasers vs finishers).
  - Physical injury localization: bite wounds, fractured limbs, and scarred tissue with authentic recovery timelines or permanent gait impairment.
- **M1.3: Continuous Offscreen Ecological Catch-up**
  - Unloaded regional cells advance via analytical demographic matrices that mathematically match micro-simulated predation and birth rates.
  - Planetary flora seed dispersal through jet-streams and migrating herds across regional borders.

---

## Phase 2 (Months 3–4): Social Dynamics, Cultural Transmission & Proto-Culture

### Milestones & Deliverables
- **M2.1: Kinship Networks & Dominance Hierarchies**
  - Alpha pair and breeding access rights governed by strength, display anatomy, and grooming reciprocity.
  - Sibling bonds, juvenile play behaviours that train motor coordination, and parental grief/mourning behaviours.
- **M2.2: Cultural Knowledge Transmission (Non-Genetic Inheritance)**
  - Young creatures learn foraging techniques (e.g. cracking hard seeds with rocks, digging water holes in dry riverbeds) by observing elders.
  - Regional dialects and calls: alarm calls that vary based on threat type (aerial predator vs ground stalker) transmitted down lineages.
- **M2.3: Speciation Divergence & Reproductive Isolation**
  - Natural behavioral and physiological pre-zygotic barriers: courtship dance mismatches, divergent mating calls, and habitat preference lock-in.
  - Post-zygotic sterility for distant lineage crosses, driving true taxonomic speciation events with automatic phylogenetic tree labeling.

---

## Phase 3 (Months 5–6): Planetary Climate Cycles, Chronicle Storytelling & Release 2.0

### Milestones & Deliverables
- **M3.1: Multi-Year Milankovitch Planetary Climate Forcing**
  - Glacial advances and retreats shifting snow lines, drying savannahs into deserts, and freezing continental sea straits into land bridges.
  - Mass extinction pressure events requiring lineages to adapt, migrate across latitudes, or seek refuge in geothermal caverns.
- **M3.2: Chronicle Engine & Emergent Story Generation (RimWorld-Style Narrative)**
  - Event log parsing into natural-language generational chronicles: heroic defense against predators, tragic winter famines, epochal crossings of great straits.
  - Milestone artifacts and grave markers: lineage members remember the location where revered matriarchs fell.
- **M3.3: Native Multi-Platform Deployment & Modding Architecture**
  - WebAssembly (Wasm) compute kernel for headless high-speed multi-region simulation.
  - Declarative JSON/Schema modding pipeline for community-authored biomes, anatomical parts, and ecological templates.
  - Native standalone packaging for Steam, itch.io, and macOS/Linux platforms with cloud save synchronization.
