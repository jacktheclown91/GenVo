# Ballistic jumping and connected pounce

Development pass, October 20, 2026. This is not full-game completion or creator acceptance.

## Connected behavior

`src/lineage/jumping.js` owns `ballistic-jumping/1`. At least two actual inherited spring legs can launch a body whose load they support. Load changes lift and horizontal speed; launch consumes existing food energy and breath. The shared gravity/velocity model advances even without input. Direction is committed at takeoff; arrows cannot supply midair lift or redirect the trajectory. Water and hard terrain can injure on landing; there is no teleport rescue. Landing adds a simulation-time recovery period. Normal bodily metabolism continues throughout.

The existing spring-leg precursor, draft rules, mutation operator, symmetry and parental recombination supply the anatomy. No new species restriction, achievement gate or mandatory generation count is added. Existing spring-leg artwork and upkeep are preserved. Modern pounce reach comes from the same anatomical kinematics, rather than a named species or legacy spring-axis threshold. A target-bound pounce winds up, pays its existing combat breath cost and the shared launch food cost, follows the same arc, then resolves its bite after landing. A target may escape during the committed movement.

Controlled and wild organisms use the same step function. Wildlife threat escape can launch a supported jump; archived biology uses that same simulation. Non-player jump users recover their existing breath stock through ordinary simulation time. Regional saves carry the policy, validate archive agreement and reject invalid midair state. Mid-jump and mid-pounce continuation is exact in the focused checks. Regional border travel waits for a ballistic jump to finish.

The mouse Jump button and Enter shortcut use the same availability/execution path. Flight, gliding, climbing, bracing and ground interactions cannot replace an in-progress jump. Native joints tuck into an unsupported aerial pose, stop walking, and render above a height-dependent shadow. Landing compresses the body briefly. Launch, landing and water splash have procedural cues on the existing audio graph; aerial travel no longer plays ground footsteps or leaves midair ground traffic/tracks. Historical powered-flight behavior is otherwise preserved.

## Historical continuity

New worlds opt in. Policy-free historical saves keep their earlier pounce and movement behavior; unsupported policies fail visibly. There is no silent adoption. As with gliding, bundled older evolved-world checkpoints still need a future explicit backed-up continuation-adoption flow to enable these new mechanics. Historical receipts and scenarios were not rewritten.

`tests/pounce.mjs` explicitly selects the historical controller for its original anatomy-axis fixtures and short ground-leap timing. Its assertions remain; the bank-stopping fixture now also asserts that a pounce really started. Modern physical anatomy, ballistic movement, costs, landing-before-bite and midair continuation are independently covered in `tests/jumping.mjs`.
