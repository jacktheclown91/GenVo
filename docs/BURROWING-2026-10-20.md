# Connected shallow underground traversal

New worlds record `excavated-subsurface/1`. Two actual digging forelimbs expose **Burrow** through the shared anatomical capability query. A sufficiently small, healthy, funded body can excavate on dry, deep-enough generated ground. The mouse action starts real work; it does not instantly hide the creature. Cancelling or losing support retains already spent food energy. At completion the creature enters through the resulting persistent entrance.

WASD/arrows move through connected passages. Attempting to move into adjacent soil starts another excavation if the ground, body load and floor difference permit it. Exit is possible at an actual entrance, not through an arbitrary roof. Wildlife uses the same work and movement authorities for threat refuge, finite root feeding and a connected return route. Species names confer no digging capability. Burrowing has no guaranteed survival or population outcome.

## Physical and spatial contract

`src/world/subsurface.js` owns sparse passage geometry, floor levels, entrances, roof failures and excavation receipts. `src/lineage/burrowing.js` owns anatomical support, work, movement, entry/exit, respiratory condition and state validation. Each region owns its own map. This is one shallow stratum connected to the existing surface, not a second copy of the surface world.

For a world with modeled cavities, solid terrain volume in normalized game units is:

`sum(world.terrain.elevation) - sum(open passage void)`

Void is excluded space, not another spendable material reserve. Excavating a cavity and entrance shaft deposits the actual removed volume into another real surface elevation cell. The Float32-rounded placement determines the receipt, drainage is rederived, and hydrology reads the changed terrain. Roof collapse lowers the existing surface as material fills the cavity; tiny Float32 roundoff is recorded and validated. Cumulative excavated/collapsed totals are receipts, not duplicate inventories. Existing runoff sediment transfers still conserve their own height deltas. Historical worlds have no new cavities unless explicitly opted in.

Surface and underground actors cannot interact across strata. Underground contact also requires an open direct passage; walls and closed diagonal corners block it. General reach and entrance search use the passage graph and floor constraints. Surface drinking, flight, gliding, jumping, climbing, building/caching/carrying and reproduction/care are unavailable below ground or during incompatible work. Pregnancies remain pending until the creature returns to the surface. Actual roots at the current passage cell remain edible through the existing finite plant/foraging authority; no food is generated by excavation. Surface footprints are suppressed below ground.

Intact roofed passages block direct rain and buffer temperature through the existing metabolism/exposure paths. Flooded or disconnected passages consume finite held air and eventually damage health; an entrance must connect to unflooded surface air. This is a bounded respiratory/hazard model, not a simulated underground water inventory. Combat, brace and stamina clocks continue during underground control. Normal metabolism, health and hydration costs continue.

Native creatures remain rendered in top-down view. Excavation uses alternating forelimb strokes; surface entrances and underground cutaway floors read the actual geometry. The controlled layer filters creature visibility and weather overlays. Action feedback, existing build/landing cues and the anatomy-aware Burrow/Cancel/Exit button are connected. No asset catalog entry is presented as proof of an underground ecosystem.
