# E10: Evolutionary mammalian care and tundra survival

This is a **functional first mammalian pathway**, not a new hardcoded mammal species.

## Inherited progression
The pathway needs a multi-generation **filament → fur** ancestry plus **live-womb** and a physically mounted **endothermic-core**. Milk production is a further inherited **mammary-gland** that requires both the heat-regulating ancestor and live birth. The organs cost body mass, mutation budget, upkeep and **flank socket capacity**: even with fur, a starter small torso cannot simultaneously mount all three flank organs, and a larger torso has a real survival tradeoff.

- `endothermic-core` consumes food energy in ordinary metabolism, partly countering cold temperature pressure, and uses more energy in heat.
- `mammary-gland` produces milk **on demand from the mother's existing energy and hydration**. This is neither a new food stock nor a free resource. Shortage or separation stops it. Manual child care offers **Nurse** where biologically valid.
- The actual birth-parent id is recorded **only for live-born offspring** via the existing funded newborn authority. An egg, even from a fully lactating ancestor, cannot be nursed as if delivered alive. A social adoptive caregiver can still offer existing ordinary care, not fabricate a gland.
- Weaning ends milk eligibility at 42% of adult maturity. Nursing is limited to a near-surface contact range. Milk transfer has energy conversion loss and a water cost. The mother keeps minimum reserves before nursing or sharing heat.
- All newborn animals are more sensitive to **real low-temperature cells**; actual close-contact maternal warming can buffer an infant while charging the mother a separate energy cost. Fur already reduces actual cold penalties through the pre-existing cover physics authority. Endothermy is not a universal biome immunity or infinite heating.
- A wild mammalian lineage receives ordinary parent-following/care routines even if its ancestral species did not have baked-in family care. Evolved anatomy determines this, not the taxon name.

## Deliberate limitations
Cold tundra and alpine classifications already exist; no complete tundra vegetation food web or permanent sea ice is claimed. No new named mammals, placenta model, milk genetics beyond mounted organ inheritance, hibernation, gestational seasonality or planet-scale travel yet. These belong in later functional slices. The world remains 128×128 playable tiles, with planetary macrogeography still at preview scope.

Validation is restricted to a targeted Node simulation smoke checking body plan, actual live birth, maternal milk/water debit, thermal tradeoffs, newborn exposure, remote-contact refusal, egg refusal, weaning and persistence; not a heavy regression sweep or a browser playtest.
