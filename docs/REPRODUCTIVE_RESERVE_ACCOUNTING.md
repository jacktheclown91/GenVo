# Reproductive reserve accounting

Agent-authored mechanics correction under the creator's ecological-integrity policy and updated development objective, Priority2. These are abstract simulation reserve units, not a groundwater or physiological tissue model.

A conceived litter retains energy investment actually debited from its parents. Birth can additionally debit the live mother's energy under the existing reproduction cost. Newborn energy is bounded by developmental body volume and the funded energy share. Already-conceived litters do not get canceled solely because local crowding changed; the emergency actor ceiling still limits available slots.

Birth hydration now follows the same finite-investment principle. `birthFunding` reads the living mother's nonnegative hydration budget. Each actual litter slot receives no more than an equal share of that budget and no more than82% of the species hydration reserve scaled by developmental body volume. The sum actually assigned to newborns is debited once from the mother. Empty slots receive no water; exhausted mothers cannot invent reserve or borrow negative hydration. This can leave mother and offspring vulnerable, which is a legitimate biological outcome to observe rather than rescue. Legacy pregnancies use actual current maternal hydration, without inventing an unrecorded gestational water investment.

Active tending transfers energy from real shelter food stores and parent reserves, and water from parent hydration above the existing reserve floor. The historical1.08/1.34/1.28 multipliers scale the maximum transfer per tending action, not the amount received per unit paid. Every transferred unit is funded; the existing0.55 energy effort cost is dissipated separately. Stress reduction and caregiving cooldown still apply. Passive nursing remains funded one-for-one under its earlier correction.

Controlled checks cover normal, nearly empty and zero maternal hydration for all species, exact donor/offspring accounting, developmental allocation, repeated birth rejection, save/load, active shelter/parent energy and water transfer, parental reserve floors and RNG neutrality. The hungry-child care test explicitly hydrates that fixture so it continues testing food priority; the thirsty-child fixture and all assertions remain.

GenVo8 now has a shared brood profile of two to four offspring, chosen once at conception and persisted with its pregnancy. The per-offspring reproductive cost is17 units: a two-offspring brood retains the earlier34-unit litter cost, while three/four cost51/68. Actual conception reserve debits retain the existing maternal35% and paternal12% shares of that brood cost. The birth payment is bounded by actual maternal energy; a depleted mother cannot borrow reserve. Offspring energy/hydration stay within their funded shares and developmental body limits. This is a shared species strategy for player and wild GenVo8, not a player-only survival bonus. Other species strategies remain unchanged.

Existing pregnancies without a planned litter retain the prior birth-time1–2 range and34-unit cost; later profile changes cannot replace an already stored count/cost. A committed brood does not guarantee offspring maturity, unrelated mates, colony establishment or starting-species survival. Family care, food, water, crowding, physical travel and death remain real costs and constraints. No carrying, mating or survival threshold is raised to achieve a desired population.
