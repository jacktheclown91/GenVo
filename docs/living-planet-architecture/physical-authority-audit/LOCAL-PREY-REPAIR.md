# Local food opportunity repair

The source-backed physical-authority audit exposed a nonlocal population dependency in current hunter breeding. The former preyNormalization scanned every living eligible animal in the world, chose the largest adult reference, and otherwise fell back to summed registry part weights. That reference converted local aquatic nutrition into adult-prey equivalents. It supplied no food directly, but a distant actor could nevertheless change local resource opportunity and its breeding gate.

## Before and after evidence

nonlocal-prey-reference-before.json retains all nine declared stock levels. One initialized eligible adult more than84 tiles away was toggled present/absent in a controlled diagnostic. Local animal prey count, accessible water-food survey, world stocks and RNG were identical. At stock0.01 and0.02, the old local gate flipped from blocked to allowed solely because of that remote presence. Eight nonzero stock levels changed resource value. No simulation updates, actual ecological deaths, reproduction or natural outcome frequency is claimed by this counterfactual.

The repaired authority uses only local reachable remaining edible tissue, weighted by the same diet preference used by meals, plus the complete accessible food survey, including usable land carrion. Both are expressed in existing nutritional carcass-equivalent food units, with the existing34 nutrition-per-unit conversion. Diet-weighted foodUnits are potential food energy, not physical tissue mass, guaranteed capture, kg or a material-conservation ledger. No part-registry weight, world-wide adult maximum or remote population supplies the denominator. The historical DEMAND_REVIEW flag still leaves metabolic competitor weighting OFF. Its former carrion-survey guard is removed independently: actual reachable carrion now contributes to food opportunity for any mouth able to eat it. This is a declared finite-food omission repair, not adoption of the separate metabolic weighting proposal. No tissues, food, energy, water, newborns or preferred populations are created.

Existing minPrey numerical values are retained, but their semantics deliberately change: they are nutritional food-unit requirements rather than animal headcounts or population-selected adult units. Hunter-lifestyle value4 is likewise retained numerically. This is an explicit biological opportunity-policy change, not a claim that the old ecological thresholds are semantically unchanged. Juvenile size and already-consumed tissue now affect actual opportunity; potential food does not guarantee capture, consent, funding or survival. Physical quantities/densities and their own future review remain separate.

local-food-final-counterfactual.json reports zero changed resource values and zero gate flips for the same stock sweep. The regression suite compares every returned local breeding-state field across remote presence/absence, retains actual finite-food/access checks, checks juvenile versus adult food value and spent flesh, preserves RNG, rejects unknown policy versions, and proves complete12-update continuation.
