## Quantity and authority rules

There must be one authority for actual owned matter. An inherited body description specifies structure and target capacities, not an automatically realized stock. Reviewed adult net volumes and material densities define target tissue quantities. The existing growth-cubed curve may request construction; it cannot simultaneously create actual volume and multiply already funded tissue again. Actual body mass must eventually sum realized tissue material and separately owned contents. Renderer/UI read those results rather than supplying mass through pixels or species names.

Each mounted structure excludes its mounted children and stored contents. Host torso excludes separately owned head and organs; host head excludes separately mounted mouth/eyes. The existing implicit head is counted once until explicitly replaced. Muscle/support/organ/covering are ownership partitions, not a universal bone skeleton. Materials can include different support types, but their densities and composition need authored evidence and review. Registry mutation cost, registry abstract mass, sprite dimensions, ancestry label and reserve percentage cannot silently become SI volume.

Tissue removal changes only the actual structures removed. Material already allocated to living tissue must have an explicit disposition through loss, injury, death or developmental resorption. A changed target in an offspring is not an instant loss of its parent's material. Bilateral and other developmental symmetry remain inherited rules, not a biological demand that every lineage have four legs. General alternative propulsion/feeding/sensing viability remains separately required rather than being waived by this contract.

## Feeding, construction and reproduction

Food quantity, nutritional energy and water are independent quantities. Every food/crop/kernel/carrion/aquatic/carry/cache transfer must name the stock owner and units. Conversion needs explicit edible composition, assimilation, waste and dissipation. Current abstract biomass and energy ratios are not automatically kilograms or joules. Producer synthesis needs declared water/mineral/carbon sources and boundary flows; a finite game field alone is not complete elemental conservation.

A construction transaction consumes assimilable material and metabolic energy, credits realized tissue, and records waste/heat/water destinations. Insufficient material or energy delays/changes development through declared biology, not a silently completed organ or a successful-mutation reroll. Target adult anatomy and currently realized juvenile tissue stay distinct. Chronological time, developmental progress and reproductive maturity must be disentangled when replacing today's age-based growth model; freezing a developmental clock is not proof of a complete life-history physiology.

Embryos/eggs must own paid material and energy during development. Their gestating parent cannot also count that matter as somatic tissue. Birth/hatching moves ownership without making or double-charging matter; failed embryos, abandoned eggs and parental death have explicit material destinations. Frozen conception determinants preserve actual inherited quantities and identity, including different-bodied parents. Guided and autonomous inheritance use the same construction authority. Heritable variation remains independent of environmental usefulness.

Movement, support, attack, reserve capacity, heat exchange, sensing and maintenance consume the resulting functional anatomy/quantities. Removing a storage organ must lose its actual capacity; adding insulation/flight/sensing must eventually change observable behavior under the corresponding environment. Density and energy policies cannot become hidden population targets. This is the full F/E interface, not completion through a few scalar bonuses.
