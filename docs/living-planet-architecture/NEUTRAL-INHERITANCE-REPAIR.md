# Neutral inheritance repair: concrete review scope

October 17, 2026. Design/audit continuation of the approved architecture. A0/A approval did not approve specific new anatomical routes. This document proposes a bounded repair before those routes are implemented; no runtime biology was changed in this audit.

## Observed violations

`accessibility-experiment/pressure-counterfactual.json` runs the real funded-birth authority from identical parental snapshots and initial RNG, changing only a synthetic exposure readout. Newborn anatomy, expressed adaptations, body and random draw progression differ. Predation produces barbed-limb expression, dryness produces water-sac expression. Absence of pressure can also change the later structural draw because the pressure path consumes different RNG; a neutral case happening to produce webbing does not make the mechanism unbiased. This diagnostic is deliberately synthetic, not an ancestry demonstration.

`heredity.js` ranks useful adaptations by parental exposure, nudges matching newborn anatomy and ability axes, sets an exposure-dependent mutation probability, and selects among high-ranked useful traits. `proto-speciation.js` selects a new trait from local pressure differentials and changes inherited choice/strength according to pressure support; its generation-four eligibility is also an arbitrary progression gate. The body-plan `mutatePlan(rng,plan)` operator itself has no exposure input. `Simulation.spawnAnimal` additionally selects different continuous-gene inheritance policies according to player lineage membership. These prevent the required shared-rule player/autonomous acceptance.

A second authority conflict is visible in the real webbing candidate: applying webbed parts raises the shared waterBody axis, expressing both swimming and water-sacs despite no storage-sac part. Useful function must not be inferred from an unrelated threshold without a supporting inherited structure. Part tissue mass/development costs remain incomplete; this audit cannot certify biological costs merely from an energy debit during movement.

## Proposed implementation boundary

1. One inherited-variation authority consumes parental inherited state, legal developmental constraints and a deterministic random source. It must not accept parental exposure, population need, current habitat, player selection status or lineage-generation gates as instructions for useful inherited changes. Existing pressure measurements remain for actual survival/behavior and observation.
2. Keep current legal part recombination and structural variation eligibility. Do not unlock flight, insulation, marine physiology, legless bodies or other new routes as part of this repair. Preserve existing trait-bearing historical organisms and parental inheritance snapshots; do not strip organs or rewrite ancestors to make the new model pass.
3. Separate inherited determinants from expression and observation. Environment may affect paid development, condition and behavior; such changes must not silently rewrite germline state. Any inherited continuous change is unbiased and bounded under an explicit versioned policy, with the same rule for wildlife and player descendants. Exact numerical policy and draw order must be included in the implementation review, not inferred from desired emergence rates.
4. Reconcile every legacy adaptation against its actual inherited determinant. Webbing does not supply a storage organ. Existing anatomy-only determinants need an explicit migration mapping or must remain honestly classified as legacy physiology until replaced; do not convert them into arbitrary new organs. Keep a single phenotype/capability derivation shared by gameplay, renderer and inspector.
5. Population divergence records inherited differences that survive and reproduce. It cannot create an advantageous trait because the population needs it. Remove pressure-ranked reinforcement and generation unlocks from inheritance, retain pressure as outcome context, and preserve recorded history with its original rule provenance.
6. Save compatibility needs an explicit policy migration record. Loading preserves completed organisms, resources, ancestry and RNG. Historical replay and normal migrated play must be distinguished; never claim exact old continuation after changing the mutation policy. Do not retain an invisible useful-mutation mode for normal wildlife while using neutral rules for the player.

## Required proof before acceptance

- Counterfactual paired births: same parents and initial random state produce identical inherited determinants when only exposure history, population pressure, camera position, or player control identity changes. Physical expression may differ only through explicitly modeled environmental development with paid costs, not germline editing.
- Recombination and legal mutation retain prerequisites and viable intermediate structures. Keep normal mating acceptance, compatibility, gestation, parental investment and development. No boosted rates or forced conception to reach a feature by day 100.
- Save migration preserves existing phenotype/history; new-policy resume is exact. Cover dead-parent inheritance, egg snapshots and old saves, with honest separation of historical replay.
- Re-run unchanged relevant mechanism/invariant checks; revise any expectation of pressure-directed benefit only through a documented biological contract correction. No skipped assertion or larger performance ceiling.
- Repeat the current real-gestation webbing route and require the descendant to survive, mature and reproduce with a compatible unrelated organism. Inspect actual traversal, energy/material consequences and visual phenotype. Add a viable simplification case after its developmental grammar is approved.

This repair is not the complete E-G pass or an anatomical route catalog approval. The 79-part catalog and accessibility audit remain required review material; world expansion remains separately gated.

## Bounded implementation candidate for creator approval

The next repair pass would retain the existing continuous-gene policy from CFG.heredity (segregation0.7, ordinary zero-centered mutation +/-0.045, larger-mutation probability0.07 and zero-centered magnitude +/-0.1, bounded by GENETIC_DOMAIN) and apply it independently of player control/lineage membership. These are existing proposal coefficients, not calibrated evolutionary frequency claims. Structural mutation retains its current0.25 chance and existing legal prerequisite/socket/symmetry operators; the pass does not add parts, removal operators or new endpoints.

Remove exposure-ranked newborn anatomy drift and emergence from the active birth authority. Existing inherited anatomy determinants retain the existing single-parent carry0.72 and two-parent inheritance behavior, without useful-direction nudges. Anatomy-only legacy traits remain inherited, explicitly marked legacy physiology; this pass must not manufacture a new physical organ to justify them. Reconcile new part-driven capability expression against actual contributing structures; webbing must not create storage-sac anatomy through waterBody alone. Existing organisms are not stripped or cosmetically rewritten.

For proto-traits, retain only inherited parental trait determinants: choose a parental determinant without reading pressure support (uniform parent choice when both contribute), retain its inherited strength and provenance, and record population location/pressure solely as observation. Remove the generation-four gate and pressure-caused origin/reinforcement. A new proto-function without a reviewed inherited developmental source remains unavailable until its real route is implemented; this is an honest mechanism gap, not permission to permanently reduce E-G scope.

Save a neutral-inheritance rules version and an explicit migration receipt. Completed old organisms, resources, parent snapshots, ancestry and history remain untouched; subsequent births use the new canonical policy. This intentionally changes future offspring relative to historical useful-mutation rules and must be visible in the migration record. Exact new-policy save continuation is required. Bit-identical replay of the old directed model requires the preserved historical source/build, not an invisible second live rule for some organisms.

Acceptance for this repair includes neutral counterfactual births across exposure/control/population contexts, no storage-sac fabrication from webbing, ordinary maturation/reproduction, inherited legacy-trait continuity, dead-parent/egg/save migration, and focused invariant/integration checks. It is separate from new tissue physiology, organ loss, flight, planetary generation and world-size expansion. No commit, push or release is included.
