## Mutation, selection and ancestry conflicts

`heredity.js:emergentAdaptation` reads parental pressure, directly nudges matching newborn anatomy and weights useful adaptations by those pressures. `proto-speciation.js` can seed pressure-matched traits. Those are directed inheritance, not selection acting on independently arising variation. Pressure remains observational. Natural mutation must sample reachable inherited changes independently of the current problem; plasticity or mutagen effects require separately specified physical mechanisms.

`evolution-draft.js` uses MP, discovery discounts and relic unlocks; `evolution-budget.js` rewards activities/births with bonus MP. These are creative progression rules, not tissue costs. Player guidance must remain inside true inherited precursors, development funding and reproductive opportunity; rewards cannot make otherwise impossible anatomy viable. Old rules need explicit versioned save compatibility rather than silent conversion.

`body-inheritance.js` largely mutates by addition/replacement. Four-leg/eye checks and a fixed radial torso constrain alternative viable forms. Founder body-effect normalization makes identical anatomy depend on the legacy species context. `wild-bodies.js` normally starts advanced predatory/armored/aquatic specialists without recorded ancestral generations. Such bodies need genuine ancestry fixtures for established-world validation; they are not proof of a simple young founding world.

No count of generations, births, biodiversity or body parts is a natural success requirement. Simplification and complete extinction remain valid. Future advanced fixture records must contain real reproduction/development history, not just a final body with a fabricated parent ID.
