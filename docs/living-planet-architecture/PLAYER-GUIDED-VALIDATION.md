# Autonomous and player-guided evolution validation

Creator directive, October 17, 2026. This expands the evaluation contract without interrupting authorized A0/A work. It does not authorize a new long trial before the essential mechanisms are integrated.

## Two distinct evaluations

**Autonomous Ecosystem Trial:** observe unassisted wildlife adapting, reproducing, interacting and sometimes failing. Record opportunity, inheritance, resource and population history without requiring a particular evolutionary outcome.

**Player-Guided Evolution Trial:** evaluate what a skilled player can achieve through planning, selective mating, preserving variants, migration, habitat engineering and legitimate editor choices. Faster specialization can be valid strategy. Do not tune progression solely to unattended wildlife or impose arbitrary generation gates to slow players.

Both trials use the same physiology, developmental rules, material and energy costs, compatibility, reciprocal mate acceptance, inheritance and environment. Player intent does not guarantee acceptance, viable offspring, useful mutations or survival. Clearly attribute permitted guided variation and spontaneous mutation. An editor must not fabricate missing precursors or bypass developmental eligibility; do not increase mutation rates to manufacture a demonstration.

## Independent scenario dimensions

| Ancestry scenario | Autonomous evaluation | Player-guided evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Young world with simple viable founders | Observe early ecological and inherited variation | Test discoverable early strategies and legitimate specialization |
| Established world with verified ancestry | Observe mature interactions and persistence | Test advanced choices and consequences available through that history |

An established fixture needs documented legitimate ancestry; injecting a finished advanced organism is a mechanism fixture, not proof of its evolutionary accessibility. The two 100-day evaluations are categories of assessment, not a requirement that all advanced routes emerge in either run.

## Evidence required before acceptance

Record seed, starting ancestry, rules, player actions, editor eligibility and actual resulting genomes. Trace successful and unsuccessful conceptions, intermediate survival, development, costs and environmental consequences. Compare opportunity and achieved function without requiring matched outcomes. Show that strategic options can be discovered in the actual UI, and inspect their movement, sensing, survival, reproductive and visual consequences in runtime.

Current A0/A page-seam evidence is a controlled movement/save fixture. It proves neither evaluation, an authentic evolutionary branch, nor ordinary gameplay through day 100. Silent computational birth clipping remains a blocking validity issue before interpreting population outcomes. Accessibility gaps remain tracked in EVOLUTIONARY-ACCESSIBILITY.md.
