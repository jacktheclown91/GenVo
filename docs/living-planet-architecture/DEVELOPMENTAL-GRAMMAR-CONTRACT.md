## Full grammar contract retained

A heritable developmental description must support repeated modules, regional identity, bilateral and other symmetry, duplication, modification, specialization and loss. Renderable part nodes are the resulting phenotype; a catalog ID is not the genome or a predefined animal class. Stable inherited module identity must survive duplication, deletion, recombination and save restoration without reindexing unrelated organs or corrupting parent records.

One developmental authority must resolve precursor identity, attachment, tissue supply, support, feeding, sensory and reproductive requirements. One physiology authority consumes the resulting tissues. Renderer/UI read that result. Player choices and autonomous mutations use the same developmental eligibility, funded growth and inheritance rules. Parent snapshots at conception include the actual developmental determinants; later parental changes cannot rewrite an embryo.

Mutation samples inherited variation independently of local usefulness. Environmental state supplies material, development conditions and selection, not a requested trait. Failed developmental changes need a declared biological outcome and accounting; repeatedly rerolling until a useful viable body appears is not an acceptable hidden rescue. Computational refusal is separately reported, never natural selection.

No blind, legless, segmented, flying or marine body is declared viable merely by deleting the present four-leg/eye checks. Those alternatives need actual propulsion/support, accessible food and sensing, respiration/regulation where applicable, paid growth and reproduction. Conversely, once those mechanisms exist, animal-class or arbitrary generation restrictions cannot forbid them.
