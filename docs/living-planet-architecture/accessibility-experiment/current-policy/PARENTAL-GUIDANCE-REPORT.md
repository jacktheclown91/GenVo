## Problem and change

The mate panel still converted anatomy carry thresholds into percentages of offspring expressing an adaptation and asserted that both parents meant all young. Physical recombination/development can produce another body, so these values were not a valid guarantee of functional offspring. The display also called an anatomy synchronizer while building its explanation.

The panel now uses `parentalAdaptationText`, a read-only interpretation of expressed parental adaptations. It attributes each observation to you, the partner or both; includes canonical benefit/tradeoff copy; and explains that young may inherit a different body and abilities. Parents without expressed adaptations still allow new inherited variation. Missing historical labels do not throw or invent anatomy. The numerical trait helper remains available to biological/legacy callers; this presentation no longer treats its output as a phenotype forecast or calls it while inspecting parents.
