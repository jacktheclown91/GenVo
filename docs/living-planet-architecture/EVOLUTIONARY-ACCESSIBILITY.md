# Evolutionary accessibility audit: initial evidence and required experiments

This is distinct from the 79-part inventory. None of the missing major routes is declared proven merely because its endpoint is described in the proposal. Specific new anatomical route implementation is not authorized by the A0/A review.

## Accessibility proof contract

For each route retain: actual ancestral genotype/phenotype; legal developmental operators and attachment checks; parental/recombination records; original mutation policy and seeds; each surviving intermediate's growth/resource costs and reproductive eligibility; actual births and descendants; final functional measurement and inspected gameplay/appearance; a viable simplification/loss branch. Identify inaccessible intermediates, extremely low opportunity/probability, incompatible recombination and lack of environmental resources rather than collapsing all failures into a catalog check.

Symbolic prerequisite reachability is only the first filter. Controlled normal reproduction/development establishes actual accessibility. A targeted lineage experiment may supply a disclosed plausible environment or valid player choice, but may not inject the endpoint, manufacture parentage, waive costs, force conception, or inflate mutation to meet day 100. Keep unsuccessful attempts and distinguish targeted accessibility from ordinary-world frequency. Selection favors realized variation; parental pressure must not instruct useful mutations.
