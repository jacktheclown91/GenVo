## Implemented contract

Materials have explicit stable identifiers. Owners hold per-material counts and separate energy counts. Counts are canonical decimal integer strings; internal arithmetic uses BigInt, with no rounding tolerance, floating-point clipping or silent conversion from existing game meters. The caller must declare reduced rational mass quanta in kg and energy quanta in J. No physical quantum, density, initial tissue quantity or calorie-to-joule conversion is supplied by default. Test quanta/counts are synthetic examples, not proposed biological calibration.

A transaction names its event identity, reason, optional empty custody owners and transfers. The kernel aggregates all outgoing obligations and checks every opening stock before applying any of them. Incoming credits cannot finance simultaneous outgoing obligations from empty owners. Successful calls return a new ledger; failures return the original unchanged reference. A rejected multi-material/energy request does not debit reserves, allocate tissue, create an owner or consume an event identity. An empty owner alone is a location for future custody, not a functional organ or an organism.

Every committed transfer preserves each declared material total and the declared energy total exactly. Energy expenditure must name a destination such as an explicit heat owner; it is not silently destroyed. Material transfers retain their material identity: the kernel cannot manufacture protein from energy or rename material through a transfer. Reviewed transformations, assimilation and reaction laws remain separate work.

Repeated identical events return the existing receipt without another debit. Transfer ordering and equivalent split edges normalize to the same intent. Reusing an event identity with a different intent fails explicitly. Receipts and quantities survive native JSON save/restore without BigInt serialization loss; unknown schemas, invalid quanta, negative/fractional/ambiguous counts, unknown references and malformed histories reject. Object-prototype names are not mistaken for stocks or already-applied events.

## Live integration still required

One authoritative World must own the adopted ledger and commit it together with biological changes. A pure atomic ledger result is not yet an atomic World birth or organ-development transaction. The World must guard against stale concurrent results, preserve stable inherited module/owner identities, and deduplicate phenotype creation as well as payment. Invalid references, event conflicts or computational pressure must not be recast as biological selection or silently suppress funded births.

Initialization is only an explicit new-world inventory or reviewed legacy conversion, never an arbitrary stock refill at birth. Genomes specify target structures/quantities; actual realized stock must come from funded construction. Missing nutrients can delay or alter development through declared biology; they cannot reroll mutations until useful/affordable. Target volume cannot be counted as realized tissue or multiplied by developmental growth twice. Rendering, movement, support, reserves, sensing and reproductive consequences must eventually read funded anatomy.

Recipes and material definitions need reviewed quantities, density/composition, energy/heat/waste accounting, unit conversion, quantization/residual handling and producer/environment boundary flows. The present game energy/biomass meters are not automatically joules or kilograms. Historical bodies, actual pregnancies, ancestry, stocks and RNG need explicit versioned migration and exact save checks. Actual paid birth, juvenile development, repair, death, decomposition, carrying and caching remain to be integrated and proven.
