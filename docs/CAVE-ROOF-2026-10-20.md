# Span-dependent cave roof support

New underground geometry records `world.subsurface.roofPolicy = span-supported-cave-roof/1`. `src/world/cave-roof.js` owns a read-only support query shared by simulation and native presentation. Existing regions without the marker retain their original thickness-only collapse behavior, including their exact recorded geometry. Unknown policies are rejected on active and archived save validation.

## Physical integration

The model uses actual surface elevation, passage floor and excavated void to derive remaining roof thickness. A topology-revision cache measures distance through open cells to solid supports; `2 * distance - 1` approximates unsupported width. Natural rock and excavated soil have different dimensionless strengths, weakened by existing local moisture. A quadratic thickness/span support proxy and minimum remaining thickness determine instability. These are explicit game-unit constitutive assumptions, not calibrated engineering units or a full elasticity/arch model. No new rock, water, debris or energy inventory is invented.

`refreshPassages` decides failures against one topology snapshot, then closes the actual passages through the existing finite material ledger. Collapse fills void and lowers the surface by the corresponding volume, retaining roundoff accounting. Subsequent structural ticks see the resulting solid supports. Initial natural geology settles through this same authority within its existing single derived-hydrology batch. Partial undercuts retain their prospective-passage clearance safety contract as well as modern support constraints.

Normal Simulation ticks report an occupied-cell collapse through existing player feedback and a native land/impact cue. The closed geometry already blocks traversal, cave sight and routed sound; blocked or disconnected occupants use the existing finite-air hazard. No arbitrary instant death or new damage rule was added. Stressed areas display stress cracks and a local Roof under strain cue from the same live support query, behind ordinary sensory fog. The renderer does not decide failure.
