The existing score, ritual appeal, rival penalty, clamps and two attempt-specific reception draw keys remain. For an eligible chooser, the added chance is `(0.70 + existing same-taxon affinity) * (1 + inherited similarity allele)/2 * bounded perception * (1 - existing appearance distance)^2`. Both parties must pass the shared `partnerAvailability` assessment before any advantage is awarded. Its optional courtship profile reads the existing ordinary display health/anatomical energy thresholds; colony anatomy retains its stricter existing encounter profile, independent of player control. This assessment limits only the new bonus, not final conception. Autonomous search also skips candidates refused by the existing `canMatePair(..., {ready:true})` authority so an exhausted look-alike cannot hide a receptive divergent candidate. Same-taxon affinity is the existing 0.01 soft tendency, read from the actual evolutionary tree. It never gates gene flow. Reception is identical for player and autonomous creatures.

Inherited genotype and candidate ranking are unchanged. Negative alleles reduce the reward; the strongest contrast allele receives none and retains identical old outcomes. Weak tastes can be outweighed by general resemblance familiarity, while strongly unusual tastes still favor divergence. Signed body tastes and ritual appeal retain their original contributions. This adds a soft influence rather than a new deterministic mate selector. The existing cap stays 0.94 per individual, maximum combined 0.8836; success is never guaranteed.

## Observed gameplay consequences and limits

Four paired actual procedural planets ran 24 days with the normal 30-second survival clock and AUTO. No animals, food, reserves, anatomy or pressures were injected. Historical reception produced 34 completed displays, 7 accepted displays/conceptions and 2 total births; balanced reception produced 10 displays, 5 accepted displays, 5 conceptions and 1 total birth. All eight worlds restored and continued exactly. Earlier acceptance legitimately changes subsequent biological RNG chronology; different policy arms are not expected to remain in lockstep. Reception itself adds no shared ecological RNG draws.

For equal controlled encounter opportunities, typical founder near/divergent acceptance ratios strengthen from about 1.28:1 to 2.63:1; inherited profiles strengthen from about 1.26:1 to 2.65:1. Thus resemblance increases relative within-phenotype assortment while still allowing cross-phenotype acceptance. These are reception-rate ratios, not measured migration or speciation rates.

This small field sample does not establish long-term balance or improved survival. No naturally recognized taxon appeared in this short window. The unchanged demanding recognition contract passes with the new policy enabled, and actual paid cross-taxon conception plus finite-funded offspring pass in a controlled registered-taxon fixture. This proves retained recognition and gene-flow possibilities, not natural speciation incidence or equilibrium. Better reception does not promise offspring: encounters, individual choice, compatibility, costs, resources and survival remain limiting.

A prototype blanket illness/water restriction at final conception was rejected because it would remove legitimate infected-receiver mating and disease transmission. Final conception, reproductive compatibility and disease authorities remain unchanged. Ill receivers receive no resemblance bonus; a canonical paid mating/disease fixture produces exactly the same reserves, disease outcome and ecological RNG with either policy.

Focused tests cover role parity, exact repeated draws, display costs, signed body tastes, strong contrast, nonuniversal reception, kinship, maturity, pregnancy, illness, energy/water condition, inherited reproductive incompatibility, save/load, prepared regions and actual cross-taxon offspring.
