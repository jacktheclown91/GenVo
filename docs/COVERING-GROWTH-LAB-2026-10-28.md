# Covering fit and developmental-age preview

Native fur, feathers, filament tufts and shells now follow the torso's existing growth and inherited axis deformation about their unchanged dorsal pivots. Previously these overlays stayed at a fixed adult size while the torso changed, obscuring juvenile and long/broad body proportions. `covering-fit.js`, `part-sprites.js` and `genvo-draw.js` share the correction at 32/64/128. It changes covering paint only: no biological size gene, growth curve, footprint, movement, contact coordinates or source artwork is introduced or modified. Seasonal density remains separate. Rigid shell paint does not inherit transient breathing/gestational inflation.

The existing Creature Lab now offers **Age (% of maturity)**. Its isolated preview uses canonical `developmentFor` and the same body/limb/sensory growth factors as gameplay. Adult framing stays fixed so newborns are visibly smaller. Pinning a reference retains its age and anatomy; the current preview can change age beside it without silently resizing the reference. Socket guides now receive the same growth/stage inputs as the actual drawn body. The slider does not age a saved animal, change a body-plan export or unlock mutation.

Implementation: `tools/creature-lab/development-preview.js`, `assembly-ui.js`, the native covering fit and existing `genvoAnchors` pose support. Original native32 and optional64/128 assets, pigmentation, frame timing and default resolution remain intact. This advances C2/C7 and existing developmental presentation; it is not the C4 giant-size implementation.
