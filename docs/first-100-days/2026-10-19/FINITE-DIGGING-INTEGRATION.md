# Canonical finite digging: integration in progress

The first canonical installation follows the successful fixed-input 100-day baseline. It is not a new ecological acceptance receipt. The pre-change runtime is preserved in the local checkpoint manifest as pre-finite-digging-runtime-2026-10-19.zip. Staged candidates remain attributable experimental evidence and are not imported by gameplay.

## Current behavior

New Simulation instances record finite-local-root-food/1. PartAbilityController selects existing compatible marshroot or dune-bulb tissue through ForagingController.options/consume. It excludes kernels, requires actual mounted digging limbs and a compatible mouth, refuses water/airborne/perched use, and records soil work/cooldown only after a positive producer debit. Normal feeding owns nutrient arithmetic, tissue/coat handling, seed/genotype effects and food-water policy. No richness reward, free water, mound multiplier or calendar-created food remains under this policy. This is current represented root/bulb food, not a completed underground compartment or unique food niche.

An autonomous animal can use its mounted limbs when its selected food is a root meal in its current cell. Other nearby foods keep their ordinary priority. Availability, shared action, ordinary feeding and the existing soil authority remain separate owners; no population or mutation target is introduced.

Mound markers are clues rather than reserves. Legacy counters are preserved; successful finite meals record finiteHarvests and lastFiniteHarvestDay separately. Discovery notices and the field guide describe the current rule. The default interaction hint still prefers ordinary represented food when available; digging remains an explicit contextual choice.

## Historical and regional boundaries

Missing-policy saves retain exact absence and legacy-patch-reward/1 behavior. Unknown tags, contradictory or invalid migration history, invalid finite visit counts and mixed regional policies are rejected. Historical calendar conversion remains covered by its explicitly legacy test.

The exported enableFiniteDigging API requires a nonempty source and records a single future-action migration without reconstructing past food flows. It preserves bodies, genomes, stocks, RNG and pending births. It is idempotent. Adoption updates current and canonical archived regions atomically only when their normal clocks are synchronized. Environmental-only archives remain unsupported for adoption and must retain legacy rules until a separate clock conversion is verified. Region crossings preserve both policy and historical absence; ordinary restore dispatches the same rule for remote simulations.

The API is not yet a usable player opt-in interface. Do not silently adopt historical saves or advance/reorder biology merely to satisfy its synchronization guard. New worlds do not need adoption. Safe UI scheduling, actual remote finite-action evidence and supported historical clock conversion remain unfinished.
