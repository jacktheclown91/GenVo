Total population reconciled with actual births/deaths; living identities and actor fields were valid; crop, freshwater food and carrion stocks stayed finite/nonnegative at the diagnostic's checks. No survival, biodiversity or evolved-organ quota was imposed.

The largest observed absolute daily water closure was 0.000008162 water-index units, below the unchanged weather test's additive 0.001 tolerance. Milestone samples are at most 40 completed days apart and include maxima over up to 64 daily budgets, so their overlapping histories cover this horizon. The initial sample has no completed water budget. This does not establish full ecosystem material conservation, which remains unfinished.

The observed memory increase is a scaling finding, not proof of a leak. Sampled resident memory reached about 834 MB, and seed-cohort storage grew substantially. Phase timings attributed about 44.4 seconds to vegetation, 30.9 to decisions and 16.9 to perception. These instrumented totals guide the next profiling/representation work; no cohorts, rare variants or births were deleted to fit capacity.
