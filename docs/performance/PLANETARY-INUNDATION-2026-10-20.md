# GenVo runoff-funded shallow inundation (20 October 2026)

## Canonical ownership and scope

New planetary Worlds now use `environment.schema:6`, which inherits previous finite salt, water volume, snowfall, soil runoff, internal wet-cell flow and paired regional seam laws. Historical schemas 1 through 5 retain their established physics on restoration.

The only new topology authority is a **runoff-funded land-to-water transition**. On each real soil day, `routePlanetaryRunoff` first books precisely the soil-rejected rainwater and soil/groundwater overflow into `environment.overland`, moves it downhill, closes the full World water receipt, then considers a bounded number of new wet tiles. A new shallow channel requires a dry interior cell alongside genuine neighboring water with at least `0.25` units of still-owned overland water. A closed local depression without a downhill route can become an isolated shallow lake after it holds `0.65` units. Candidate water is limited to `[0,2]` and at most 8 cells per World-day can transition. Border tiles are deliberately excluded until a regional water topology agreement exists.

Each transition uses the existing `World.setSurfaceWater` and `synchronizeSurfaceWater` topology transaction, with an explicit `source:'overland'`. That source must be backed by a finite existing overland stock on the **same cell** in a schema-6 World. The transaction subtracts that quantity from `overland` before assigning the exact amount to `environment.volume`; it creates a freshwater tile with zero incoming salt, records `internalWater` in the topology event and leaves external `topology.waterInput`, `topology.saltInput`, `environment.waterInput` and `environment.saltInput` unchanged. Freshwater remains distinct from marine reservoirs.

The authoritative wet-cell mask and depth are persisted. Topography-derived drainage, land connectivity, drinking-water access and water distance are rebuilt after each successful transition; the generator's original land sample is not used to undo the new shoreline on a load.

## Conservation and ecological boundary

The sum of `environment.volume + environment.overland` does not change merely because an overland puddle becomes an open-water cell. The amount of water added earlier by rainfall is separately credited in that day's receipt. No extra organisms, aquatic resources, genomes or ecological outcome are generated by the transition. Existing terrestrial plant biomass and underlying soil stores are **not** yet remapped into a separate drowned-vegetation lifecycle; they remain existing stocks. This is an explicit remaining ecological integration step, not permission to delete tissue and seed variants.

The mechanism currently models **formation only**. It does not create new regional boundary channels, widen a river through physical erosion, move salt into new land water without a real source, lower topography, drain shallow wet cells back into land, or validate long-term 100-day coastal stability. Existing wet-water transport and regional replay continue under their original conservation authorities.

## Verification

`tests/planetary-runoff-inundation.mjs` is the targeted acceptance check. It uses a real natural coastal wet-to-dry contact, confirms that an unfunded conversion is rejected without changing topology, seeds a controlled initial owned runoff stock, advances the real daily soil and environmental physics, and verifies a real new wet tile, internally debited water, zero external credits, exact World water-budget closure, stored topology, archive restoration and deterministic biological continuation. A historic schema-5 World with identical runoff must not automatically flood.

The existing version-5 12-day rainfall baseline, version-4 internal water baseline, reciprocal regional water and salt checks, save migrations, three-region replay, browser checks and seed/cohort CI remain mandatory. Passing them does not demonstrate a realistic growing river network or the eventual transition of drowned land biota.
