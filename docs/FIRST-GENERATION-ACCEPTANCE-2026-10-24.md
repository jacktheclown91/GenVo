## 4. Summary of Verifications

### Priority A — Genuine Browser Gameplay Acceptance
Previous verifications sometimes relied on hybrid execution (`sim.update()` called synchronously or state queried directly to steer logic). This acceptance pass enforced **strict browser simulation integrity**:
- **Native Browser Game Loop**: Every frame ran through `requestAnimationFrame` and `FrameStepper`.
- **Zero Simulation Shortcuts**: `window.__frames.directUpdateCalls === 0` throughout all runs.
- **No Creature Teleportation**: All creature positions progressed strictly through the simulation's movement mechanics.
- **No Resource Injection**: No food, water, thermal buffers, or births were injected out-of-band.
- **Discoverable Native Controls**: Interactions occurred exclusively via keyboard (`WASD`, `KeyE`, `Shift+KeyE`, `KeyC`, `KeyN`, `KeyF`, `KeyH`, `KeyP`, `BracketRight`, `BracketLeft`) and mouse canvas clicks.

#### Journey 1 — First 15 Minutes (1x Speed)
- Player begins with introductory tutorial / goals card (`journey1-01-welcome.png`).
- Manual movement verified with WASD at 1x speed (`manualMoveDist: 1.783–1.827` tiles).
- Unavailable action feedback verified on `#interactionDock` (e.g. dig button shows disabled styling and informative toast when unequipped).
- Contextual interaction with world objects verified: clicking plants and water tiles moves the creature to the target and executes foraging/drinking (`journey1-02-manual-forage-drink.png`).
- Creature interaction verified: clicking a mate opens `#creatureTray`, displaying creature traits and enabling Courtship via `KeyC` (`journey1-03-creature-inspect-court.png`).
- Courtship succeeds naturally through the mutual courtship display protocol (`journey1-04-courtship-result.png`).

#### Journey 2 — Complete First Generation
- **Conception & Gestation**: Natural mating on Day 1 leads to pregnancy; 16-day gestation advances under the native 30-second day clock.
- **Natural Birth**: On Day 17 (Autumn), the litter is born without shortcuts. The modal `#youngSummary` ("Your First Young") appears automatically, pausing the simulation, and displays the newborn count, maternal traits, and care advice (`journey2-01-young-summary.png`).
- **Parental Care (`KeyE`)**: Following `#youngSummary` dismissal, pressing `KeyE` executes the contextual nurse/care action, transferring maternal reserves and lowering offspring care stress (`journey2-02b-parental-care.png`).
- **Growth & Dependency**: Offspring remain dependent until age 18.6, following the mother and feeding from maternal milk and home range foraging.
- **Winter Survival**: Offspring and mother endure Winter (Days 25–32).
- **Maturity Gate (Age 30)**: At Day 47 (Summer Year 2), offspring reach sub-adult maturity (`age: 30.0`), immediately unlocking 3 eligible heirs in `sim.lineage.eligibleHeirs()`.
- **Succession Chooser (`KeyH`)**: Pressing `KeyH` opens `#successionChooser`, displaying comparative heir cards showing each candidate's inherited body scale, sprint speed, bite damage, carry capacity, and learned habits (`journey2-03-succession-chooser.png`).
- **Generation 2 Reveal (`#transition`)**: Selecting an heir card triggers the Generation 2 Reveal modal, illustrating parent-to-child portrait comparisons, inherited traits, mutations, and lifestyle progression (`journey2-04-generation-reveal.png`).
- **The First Legacy (`#legacySummary`)**: Clicking through the reveal opens the First Legacy modal, awarding the milestone and establishing the lineage motto (`journey2-05-first-legacy.png`).
- **Manual Control of Descendant**: Clicking "Continue Evolving" transfers full player control to the Generation 2 descendant (`generation === 2`). The player manually moves with WASD and forages with `KeyE`.
- **Save & Continue Parity**: Pressing `KeyP` opens the Pause screen -> Return -> Save & Return -> Main Menu. Clicking "Continue" reloads the world and restores the exact G2 descendant ID, coordinates, energy, hydration, and legacy status (`journey2-06-g2-save-continue.png`).

#### Journey 3 — Seasonal Preparation & Winter Survival
- **Comparison Scenario (`established-valley-d30`, 1x speed, manual control)**:
  - **Prepared Strategy**: Player moves to the family home area, constructs a shelter using `KeyN` (`journey3-01-shelter-built.png`), inspects the shelter context tray (`journey3-02-shelter-tray.png`), caches `18.69` food units using `KeyF` (`journey3-03-food-cached.png`), and reinforces the weather seal to `0.99` ("Reinforced"). Entering the shelter (`KeySpace`) provides an active thermal refuge: `thermalMultiplier: 0.422` (a **57.8% reduction** in thermal energy loss during freezing winter conditions).
  - **Unprepared Strategy**: Creature remains outside without shelter (`shelterBuilt: false`, `weatherSeal: 0.0`, `thermalMultiplier: 1.0`, `foodStore: 0`).
  - **Outcome**: The prepared creature easily survives the winter with healthy energy and hydration margins, while the unprepared creature experiences severe thermal drain.
- **Live Winter Snow Hydration (Journey 3B)**:
  - During live gameplay in Journey 2 on Day 25, pressing `Shift+KeyE` under active snowpack harvests snow hydration, replenishing water reserves during freezing temperatures when surface water is locked in ice (`journey3-05-winter-snow-hydration.png`).
