# Creature face readability development review

Changed the shared head geometry and facial mounting renderer. Legacy body graphs with an implicit compatibility head now place their actual eyes and mouth on that visible head, rather than leaving facial organs on the torso. Editor anchors use the same attachment positions. Explicit modular heads retain their nested ownership; deliberate torso-faced/headless creatures retain torso sockets. Head width increased from 46% to 60% of the smaller torso dimension (minimum 3.4 presentation units). No saved graph, capability, species rule or reproductive outcome was changed.

Harsh assessment after inspecting the motion-review and actual close-world frames: faces are visibly grouped at the front and read more clearly. This is a renderer attachment correction, not completion of organic artwork. Several organisms still have a radial or pinwheel limb silhouette, sharp crowded appendage transitions, and too little body volume at normal zoom. Walking limbs need more coherent bend direction and better limb-to-torso transitions. The eight-body enlarged motion overlay is supplementary anatomy evidence; it does not replace actual world captures. Full visual acceptance and removal of fixed biological species rules remain unfinished.
