# Persistent temperature spells

New worlds record `persistent-temperature/1`. `src/ecology/thermal-weather.js` supplies independent fast and slow signed, autocorrelated temperature anomalies. They are stationary, centered on zero and seeded separately from rainfall and biological randomness. Warm/cold spells and stronger heatwave/cold-snap conditions are runs of the process, not scheduled disasters or population corrections. The Gaussian generator is the existing weather primitive; the rainfall stream and its arithmetic are unchanged.

`Simulation.phaseClimate` applies the anomaly to the existing seasonal baseline, bounded by the existing normalized temperature domain [-0.5, 1.5]. Climate exposes the actual applied anomaly after normalization. Existing local-temperature, soil evaporation, snow/thaw, plant growth, animal water use, coat/thermal balance, parental care and embryo/young-climate systems receive that climate through their existing authorities. Daily terrain/animal temperature fields retain their existing relaxation; a new air-temperature anomaly does not instantly overwrite every physical field or inject a second damage system. Thermal forcing is a normalized climate model, not a full atmospheric energy-transport simulation.

Shared local readings distinguish warm/cold spells from heatwaves/cold snaps using applied deviation and actual local temperature. Native warm/cold tint, high-temperature shimmer and status cues run in the ordinary renderer, including low-detail mode. Underground and submerged bodies use their existing stratum behavior rather than a surface thermal veil. Complete weather art/audio acceptance remains open.

Thermal clock, independent RNG, process components and anomaly persist exactly. Historical worlds keep their recorded seasonal formula unless explicitly opted into a current-rules continuation. Adoption initializes the process at the current day, not by inventing past thermal/biological history, and changes only future policy metadata. Original backup and continuation remain one atomic transaction; failed storage rolls back the state, policy and receipts with the other rule migrations. Visited archives require canonical catchup and agree on policy/provenance; actual travel transfers the active atmospheric state without duplicating physical inventories.

## Evidence and remaining work

`tests/thermal-weather.mjs` checks deterministic signed spells, exact independent-stream continuation, rainfall and biological RNG parity, canonical climate coupling, bounded extreme forcing, existing snow-funded thaw, actual simulation-tick Save/Continue, malformed state and historical absence. The climate-only multi-day clock fixture does not claim elapsed organism history. `tests/movement-adoption.mjs` covers unchanged whole-state inventories/organisms, atomic failure and real regional adoption/travel. The legacy weather test explicitly selects historical thermal absence when asserting the old climate object; its rainfall and statistical checks remain intact. The unchanged weather/soil resume check closes its 14-day water budget. All 28 focused playability checks pass.

`tools/verify-thermal-weather-browser.mjs` selects reproducible day-one thermal seeds using only the independent atmospheric process, then uses normal New World and IndexedDB Continue. It edits no body, reserve, age, weather value, terrain or simulation history. [Receipt and inspected heat/cold frames](thermal-weather-review-2026-10-20/receipt.json) preserve actual generated process values and exact restored climate. This proves generated cues and persistence; it does not prove natural lifetime survival, creator acceptance or the incremental causal effect of a spell on every ecological consumer.
