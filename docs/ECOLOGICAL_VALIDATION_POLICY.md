# GenVo8 ecological validation policy

**2026-10-12 direction clarification:** See [Emergent evolution and test contract](EMERGENT_EVOLUTION_AND_TEST_POLICY.md). No unforced seed must meet a minimum birth, digging, migration, colony, or post-interruption recovery count. Such expectations are valid only inside specifically justified controlled fixtures. The point of different seeds is the possibility of radically different endgame outcomes.


Creator direction: direct message, 2026-10-11. This supersedes universal population-count and starting-species survival requirements in earlier validation plans. Detailed implementation below is agent-authored.

A surprising ecological outcome is not automatically a simulation failure. Booms, collapses, extinction, dominance and uneven distribution may be valid. Accept outcomes through ecological integrity, causality, reproducibility, persistence and computational stability.

Investigate unusual growth or decline against actual food, water, habitat, anatomy, reproduction eligibility, mortality and carrying calculations. Preserve finite resource accounting. Record population and resource trajectories, mortality causes, migration and regional distribution. Explain uncertainty rather than converting a surprising count into either a failure or an automatic pass.

| Evidence | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| General natural-world integrity checks | Advancing simulation, finite state/resources, population accounting and consistent records; no universal survival or equilibrium assertion |
| Controlled scenario regressions | Specific mechanisms under documented fixtures; expected outcomes must be justified by those fixtures |
| Ecological review | Supported causal explanation of growth, decline, extinction and restructuring; structural checks alone are insufficient |
| Persistence/reproducibility | Exact source and reported seeds, save continuity and independent long-run evidence |
| Computational acceptance | Measured runtime/frame cost, memory and stability; population count alone cannot establish it |

The existing560 population target is a diagnostic performance expectation, not a universal ecological maximum. The900 emergency ceiling remains a runtime constraint to investigate: reaching it can censor births and cannot demonstrate natural regulation. This policy does not itself authorize removing safety limits or claim that high-population play is smooth.

Existing controlled tests for reproduction, carrying, finite freshwater resources, disease, save continuity and performance remain separate. The former natural ecology test's universal survival, recovery-cohort, disease prevalence and population limits lack a scenario-specific justification and are replaced by general integrity checks. Their original assertions and historical run receipts remain preserved.

Known defects remain defects regardless of population outcomes. The reproduced autonomous conception eligibility bypass and nursing energy creation still require correction and verification. Any carrying model adjustment must address a demonstrated mechanism problem, not target an arbitrary final count.

No rescue spawning, hidden mortality changes, resource creation, predetermined ratios or mandatory survival of starting species. A runner finishing successfully means its automated checks passed; full ecological acceptance still requires causal review and the other evidence above.

Creator direction: ordinary wildlife must support autonomous spread under suitable conditions. Human-guided player evolution is expected to outperform automatic player control. Keep these acceptance categories separate; see [Wildlife spread and player guidance](WILDLIFE-SPREAD-ACCEPTANCE.md).

Jobs already running retain their loaded code and historical acceptance semantics. Do not restart them because of this policy. Review their outcomes under this policy in a separate receipt; do not rewrite their original results. Subsequent full-suite and long-world runs use the revised checks. The full development objective, including later family lineages and biome pressures, remains active.
