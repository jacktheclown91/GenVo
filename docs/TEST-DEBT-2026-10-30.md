## Resolution pass (October 30, later)

16 of the 36 now pass; 15 joined the playable gate, with a new `starter-partner` test (231 → 247 checks). Two fixes changed game code; the rest corrected fixtures whose premises later rules had made false. No assertion was weakened, and no resource, birth or population was created to pass.

**Game defects found and fixed**
- **Unreachable starting partner** (`tutorial-start-targets`). `prepareStarterMate` took the nearest same-species animal in a straight line and moved a distant one onto any dry tile. In 4 of 8 sampled worlds the partner stood on another landmass, so the tutorial's mating step could not be completed. New games now use `starter-partner/1` (`same-land-partner/1`, in `SURVIVAL_OPT_IN`): the partner is the nearest one on the founder's own land, and a distant or stranded one is moved onto that land (the same random draws; the first bearing is tried as before). Result: 0 of 40 sampled worlds start with an unreachable or out-of-sense partner. It is applied once at creation and stores no field, so worlds built with the original options stay exactly original (the `interleaved-day-clock` original-source fixture still matches). Creature Lab starts were already correct.
- **Resident starts guided to the wrong partner** (same test). The mating guide rewrite (`98b276b2`) lost the October 25 rule that an existing-resident start does not prefer the world's default founding partner. Restored in `src/ui/mating-tutorial-guide.js` (ChatGPT: guidance logic only, no presentation change).
