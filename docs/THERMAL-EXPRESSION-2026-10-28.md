# Thermal creature expression

`src/render/thermal-expression.js` and the shared renderer now present surface cold load through lowered carriage, tucked sensory posture and narrowed eyes at stronger demand; heat load produces a smaller fatigue posture. Cold shivering requires the canonical combined fur/endothermic-core anatomy. These are read-only expressions of `thermalSiteDemand().thermal`, not new temperature, distress, refuge or metabolic authorities. Baseline core energy upkeep is excluded from the visual intensity calculation.

Signals are cached per actor/tick/plan/position. NPC poses use bounded quantized cache tokens. Only body carriage and head/sensory posture change; limb reach, stride, foot spread, movement speed, physical footprint and artwork resolution remain unchanged. Actual meals, threats, courtship, work, swimming, flight and dormancy keep priority. Unsupported underground/perched/depth contexts are excluded instead of using the surface query there. No exposure-controller state accessor, RNG, reserve charging or save-schema change was introduced.
