# Finite vegetation fire

New worlds record `finite-vegetation-fire/1`. Actual lightning proposals can ignite suitable dry standing vegetation through one shared ignition authority. No creature or desired ecological outcome selects ignition. Existing regional rain, soil moisture and planetary wind control ignition, extinction and directional neighbor spread. Spread uses stateless geographic noise, not biological RNG. Wet ground, water and unplayable terrain cannot ignite. Ordinary physical plant growth remains the only regrowth path.

Each burning tick debits existing Float32 land-producer stocks exactly within its requested allowance. Existing crop-loss hooks scale actual reproductive tissues and export the corresponding stored producer energy. No litter, seeds or replacement vegetation is minted. The existing normalized biomass/mineral convention returns 0.1 mineral per biomass consumed to the existing mineral owner; the remaining 0.9 is explicitly recorded as atmospheric export. The saved ledger satisfies burned = mineralReturn + atmosphericExport. These are provisional resource units, not elemental carbon, combustion oxygen or thermodynamic energy closure.

Exposed bodies receive actual health damage and existing injury/mortality/carcass behavior, with environmental cause attribution. Intact cave roofs, airborne bodies and submerged bodies are shielded by the present coarse surface-fire model. Fire wakes torpor and bypasses ordinary wildlife decision refresh throttling. Autonomous creatures choose an actually visible, occupiable, reachable unburning location; manual player input remains authoritative. Native flames share sensory visibility; crackle shares anatomical hearing and medium routing. The renderer and audio cannot ignite, grow or consume vegetation.

Fire cells and cumulative outputs belong to their physical region. Travel transfers policy, not foreign burning coordinates. Ordinary regional biology advances archived fires. Snapshots copy the small mutable fire record and cell rows; restore validates active/archive clocks, cell geometry, intensity and output closure. Historical missing-policy saves remain unchanged. Historical adoption UI is unfinished.
