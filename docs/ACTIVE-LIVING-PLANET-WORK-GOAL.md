## Governing objective
Finish the biological and planetary *possibility engine*, not a collection of predefined animal classes or selected outcomes. Begin young worlds with viable, relatively simple ancestors. Give the player legitimate high-leverage choices in habitat, reproduction and lineage selection. Permit specialization, simplification, high biodiversity, collapse or total extinction through shared biological rules.

**Guarantee biological rules, not biological outcomes.**

## Live baseline, do not reimplement
The baseline already contains A0 water topology/save compatibility, current-map pages (A), opt-in B0 planetary descriptor on the original map, persistent seeded weather/soil-water, neutral inherited core traits, paired symmetry and optional-organ loss, head-structure conversion, verified newborn-to-mature-to-descendant trajectories, a versioned relative-anatomical-mass policy, bounded GPU renderer batching and material-funding kernels that remain inactive. The Oct18 fast report claims 408 passing suites, five known failures, zero new failures and 12 deferred long suites. These are reported receipts, not a new run.

## Implementation dependencies
1. **B: Physical planet**. Paged global geometry distinct from detailed local map; continental landmass, ocean bathymetry, mountains/elevation, basin-connected lakes/rivers, salinity/freshwater transition, conservation at page seams and persistent topology. Do not simply resize all n*n arrays. Connected traversable geography and discovered-only mapping.
2. **C: Climate and producers**. Persistent regional rainfall/winds, sun, seasons, rain shadows, cold/dry/saline field conditions; soil-water and salt flux; funded woody/canopy producers, light competition, plant thermal and salinity responses; no biome bonuses or ecosystem rescue.
3. **D: Regional biological history**. Same Simulation and World authorities with region indices, persistent distant individuals, genotype stocks, exact migration and cross-boundary feeding/mating, bounded scheduling, no camera-dependent biology, no capacity-clipped paid births; saved deterministic history.
4. **E: Developmental grammar**. Stable homologous module identities, axial/segment duplication and loss, alternatives to four legs and visible eyes only where a real viable propulsion/sensing pathway exists; independent inherited pigmentation, bilateral/radial/asymmetric pattern development, tissue construction and real organ contents; normal young founding ancestry and accessible precursor routes for all supported advanced organs.
5. **F: Shared physiology**. Mechanically funded growth, embryo/egg material accounting, actual tissue-derived mass and work (migrating approved policies); shared heat balance/endothermy-ectothermy continuum, insulation/fur/feather/scale/exposed skin regional coverings; finite oxygen, buoyancy, aquatic propulsion and osmoregulation; ground/climbing/burrowing, height/gliding/powered flight through actual forces; useful senses and reproductive secretions. Player and wildlife share identical authority.
6. **G: Evolution as playable strategy**. Real reproduction/partner choice, hybridization and inheritable differentiation; player-guided descendant editor bounded by ancestry and tissue budgets, meaningful family care and control, readable anatomy, pigmentation and thermal HUD based on actual state; separate informed player-guided and autonomous opportunities.
7. **H: First 100 Days of a Living Planet**. A young normally initialized world plus separate genuine-ancestry established fixture; actual human-facing play; daily causal receipts at 1/10/30/60/100; meaningful specializations but no quota for speciation or diversity. Contrast a small number of seeds only after integrated mechanisms exist.

## Progression and cost rules
A part is not a functional capability unless the registered anatomy, developmental precursor, support, growth/mass/upkeep/work, physical consumer, inherited route, save migration, renderer and accessible player observation all exist. Advanced organisms need successful reproductive ancestry, not arbitrary generation/achievement gates. Successful simple organisms and loss of complexity remain valid. New mutations are not directed by local needs. No guaranteed mate, rescues, force-spawned specialists or made-up food.

## Validation during construction
After each mechanism, run targeted deterministic tests for conservation, actual birth inheritance, player/wild parity, saves, meaningful failures and displayed actions; add Chromium checks for visual/interactions when possible. A test failure is not solved by raising ceilings or inventing ecosystem outcomes. Avoid multi-world centuries until H. Benchmark performance at current density before growing the world; screen-load caches must not change ecology.

## Integration discipline
- One canonical World/Simulation and existing renderer; no competing engines.
- Keep historical saves under explicit rule/generator versions, never reinterpret old tile arrays, genes or tissue masses silently.
- Preserve provenance and old test receipts as *history*, not present acceptance.
- Keep large generated evidence/logs out of Git; small useful reports and tests remain.
- Work on this branch until the implementation is reviewed; `main` and published releases remain unchanged. No release or force push.
- Truthful incremental status: implemented, partially integrated, targeted-tested, browser-verified, creator-approved, and full 100-day acceptance are distinct.
- The October 30 freeze is a checkpoint, not permission to claim unfinished biology complete.
