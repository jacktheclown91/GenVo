# Finite ventilated cave oxygen

New underground geometry owns `subsurface.air` with policy `finite-cave-oxygen/1`. The physical authority is `src/world/cave-air.js`; biological demand comes from `undergroundRespirators` in `src/lineage/burrowing.js`. Simulation advances transport and uptake once per canonical tick, then applies the existing finite-air survival condition. Rendering reads this same state.

## Inventory and transport

Each open passage's actual excavated void determines its gas-volume capacity. Initially generated geological cavities start with atmospheric oxygen as an explicit world initial condition. Later excavation creates empty gas volume and must receive oxygen through connected cells or an actual entrance. It does not spawn a new oxygen reserve. Partial erosion does not create occupiable air space before a passage physically opens.

Oxygen exchanges simultaneously across connected cardinal passage edges with compatible floors. A bounded diffusion step cannot overdraw a donor or oversaturate a receiver. Real dry entrances exchange with the atmospheric boundary. The current surface-water obstruction used by shared cave traversal also blocks breathing and ventilation; this is not a new groundwater or cave-water inventory. When a cavity closes, its finite remaining oxygen is accounted as expelled gas through collapsing material; volume and oxygen records are removed with the actual cavity.

The ledger records oxygen introduced, respired and exported. Save validation requires one capacity record per open passage, bounded oxygen concentration and closure of the complete oxygen balance. There is no food, water, soil, energy or decorative organism added by this system. The model tracks oxygen volume at fixed gas capacity; it does not yet solve gas pressure, nitrogen or carbon dioxide.

Biological demand scales with existing anatomical body load and current rest/excavation activity. Occupants sharing scarce air receive the same proportional adequacy, independent of actor order. Sufficient oxygen supports breathing even in a sealed cavity until it is consumed; mere connectivity to a distant entrance no longer guarantees unlimited air. Inadequate oxygen connects to the existing hypoxia/finite-air countdown and health consequences. UI feedback reports fresh, thinning or depleted local air and the remaining countdown, rather than exposing hidden organisms.

## Persistence and evidence

Historical regions without `subsurface.air` retain their recorded entrance-route breathing behavior and do not acquire a gas inventory on ordinary restore. Newly generated regions record their own initial air and retain it through regional snapshots and revisits. Existing historical geometry is not replaced. There is currently no conversion UI for adding finite gas to an already visited historical region.

`tests/cave-air.mjs` checks sealed depletion, entrance-to-interior ventilation, rock obstruction, empty new excavation, collapse displacement, oxygen ledger closure, proportional scarce-air sharing, invalid-demand rejection before mutation, real biological demand and exact next-tick active saves. Corrupt oxygen receipts and historical absence are covered. All 42 focused integration checks pass; the canonical-source audit and exact public-export parity pass.

Its optional `--air-depletion` fixture records a controlled oxygen withdrawal as external export, then verifies that the normal simulation tick consumes air tolerance and displays the native depleted-air warning. This is an explicit intervention for presentation verification, not evidence of natural respiration frequency or material-free gas disappearance. ## Incomplete work

This is a finite oxygen and diffusion-ventilation model in dimensionless game units. Wind/thermal convection, gas pressure, carbon dioxide, fire, microbial/root respiration, oxygen-specialized anatomy and complete cave ecology remain incomplete. Coupled cave seepage/drainage and multi-level geometry are separate unfinished physical systems. Natural-lifetime balance, late-session performance and creator visual acceptance remain unproven.
