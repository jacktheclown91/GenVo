# Wildlife spread and player guidance

Creator direction: balance wild creatures to spread autonomously. Expect human players to guide evolution and population success faster and more effectively than the automatic player controller.

Wildlife acceptance uses ordinary autonomous animals, their actual diets and inherited anatomy, with no player commands, supplied resources or rescue spawns. In scenario-specific habitats known to support survival and movement, verify that mechanics can support resource-funded reproduction, physical migration, settlement and subsequent reproduction; do not require those events in every seed or every lineage. Repeat across seeds and regional pressures. Record population, birth regions, occupied regions, migration arrivals, mortality and resource trajectories. A distribution snapshot or a high population alone does not prove spread.

Keep three evidence categories separate:

| Category | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Autonomous wildlife | Establish natural spread, finite survival and habitat-dependent reproduction without human assistance |
| Automatic player lineage | Diagnose the fallback controller and biological inheritance; its success rate is not the human player's balance target |
| Human-guided player lineage | Verify that informed movement, resource preparation, mate selection and ancestry-compatible evolution can improve outcomes through actual mechanics |

Human guidance may improve decisions and accelerate adaptation, but cannot bypass resource accounting, anatomy, kinship, reproductive investment or mortality. Do not boost all wildlife until it matches skilled players, or handicap players until they match the automatic controller.

Preserve genuine extinction, migration risk, predator-prey differences and seed-specific outcomes. Fix demonstrated barriers to spreading before tuning growth. Evaluate population-supported habitat occupancy and continued reproduction, rather than equal species counts or universal survival.

Current milestone observation includes an automatic player lineage and ordinary wildlife. Its automatic lineage extinction is a diagnostic outcome, not a verdict on human-guided viability or autonomous wildlife balance. Dedicated wildlife spread acceptance remains required.
