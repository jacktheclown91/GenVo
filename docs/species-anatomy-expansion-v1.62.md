# GenVo8 v1.62 — Seed carriers and shoreline hunters

The new wildlife is composed of ordinary inheritable body parts. No special breeding paths or generated food pools are introduced.

- **Podshell:** slow, defended seed-pod specialist. It eats finite thistlepod biomass and uses the existing animal-seed-cargo/seed-bank/dispersal/germination loop, carrying seeds beyond the source plant. The inherited kernel-beak increases seed nutrition while harming soft-plant efficiency. The heavy thorn-carapace reduces damage, slows locomotion, raises upkeep, and can repel a hunter. Its spikes also sting predators in close player combat.
- **Bankjaw:** web-footed shoreline predator that can cross water and prey on Rillsifters and wetland scavengers; gape-mouth can consume finite aquatic fry. A limited early population avoids replacing the Stalker or making every freshwater site lethal.

Both use canonical live birth, mate preferences, editable DNA via inherited body plans, field-guide discovery, species rendering and saved world population. There is no guaranteed colony success or predation result.

Development status: confirm tests and Chromium evidence before considering this a stable release.
