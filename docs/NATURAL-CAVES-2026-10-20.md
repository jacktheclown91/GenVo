# Natural caves and shared entrances

New worlds record `seepage-caves/1` alongside the existing underground traversal policy. Stateless rock fractures, moisture, dry terrain and drainage seed shallow connected cavities. Neighboring fractures extend a cavity rather than placing repeated adjacent entrance shafts. This is an initial geological condition, not an invented account of elapsed ecological history. Cavities remove real solid volume and place the same material in actual surface cells; biological RNG, water and food are not credited by cave generation.

Planetary dry-land runoff now funds undercutting along its actual routed edges. Eligible flow uses the existing daily terrain-erosion cap and does not also pay for surface erosion. Partial voids accumulate before becoming occupiable passages; continued undercutting can expose an entrance. Finite material transfers, partial-roof collapse and digging into partly eroded rock share the cavity/spoil ledger. Water and mineral transport retain their existing authorities. This is a shallow runoff-undercutting model, not volumetric groundwater or a chemical dissolution simulation.

Any body that fits can use an existing opening through ordinary Interact or its selected-object action. Digging organs remain necessary to excavate new ground. Entry and exit take 1.2 simulation seconds, anchor the body and charge load-dependent food energy; the existing burrowing command uses this transition when reusing an entrance. Newly excavated entrances retain their funded excavation-work transition. Wildlife can use an entrance at its position when threatened, and existing underground decisions return along connected passages. Roots, roof weather protection, disconnected/flooded air hazards and contact separation use the shared underground systems.

Surface abilities, combat, care, shelter work, reproduction and regional travel reject an unfinished entrance transition through their existing authorities. Native grip animation, descent/ascent progress, rocky entrance marks and distinct natural-rock passage floors are connected. Interact exposes entrances without inventing a digging capability. The simulation waits for the first fully prepared scene after loading, so actions do not finish behind the terrain-loading screen.

The complete geometry, partial erosion and exact actor transition persist. Historical worlds retain their recorded geology and policies; the existing backed-up continuation can opt into future cave processes and entrance traversal without generating a fictitious past. Adoption agrees across visited regional snapshots and rolls back with the other movement metadata if the atomic backup/continuation transaction fails. No original checkpoint or historical receipt was rewritten.

## Evidence and limits

`tests/natural-caves.mjs` checks deterministic initial geometry, solid material/water/RNG conservation, actual runoff routing, no double erosion work, partial erosion and collapse, partial-void digging, non-digger Interact entry/exit, conflicting action restrictions, malformed flux rejection and exact mid-entry persistence. `tests/movement-adoption.mjs` covers full-state preservation, failed-transaction rollback, original read-only backups and actual regional adoption/travel/revisit. All 26 focused playability checks pass, as do nursing conservation, egg/nest predation, weather exposure, runoff inundation, canonical-source audit and public-export parity. The final derived browser build contains 688 files and 311 source modules.

`tools/verify-natural-caves-browser.mjs` uses normal New World, a legally imported primitive sandbox founder, mouse Interact, keyboard movement and native IndexedDB Save/Continue. Only initial actor position is staged at an existing generated entrance; it does not add caves, change terrain, anatomy, energy or food. The read-only load wrapper records transition state at the normal load boundary. [Review receipt and images](natural-caves-review-2026-10-20/receipt.json) distinguish that observation from simulation time after Continue. Browser screenshots were inspected; this is automated gameplay observation, not creator acceptance.

Still incomplete: multiple underground depths, regional tunnel seams, volumetric groundwater/flood transport, cave-specialized organisms and breeding/food webs, dedicated darkness/sound perception, broader natural-life acceptance and final artwork. Surface cells currently determine flooding hazards; that is not proof of hydraulic cave water. Existing broad-suite coat/sediment fixture failures documented in `BURROWING-2026-10-20.md` remain separate. The full Creature-stage game remains unfinished.
