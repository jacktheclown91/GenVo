# Current implementation and acceptance

New games record `body-size/1` (morphology S1): every body carries an inherited, continuous adult size (0.5–2×, founders 0.95–1.05) that sets its mass (× s³), its resting cost (Kleiber M^0.75, water M^(2/3)) and its bite (× s²). Food and water are absolute outside bodies and convert at every intake and transfer site, so nothing is created or lost between bodies of different size. Saved worlds keep their rules and replay exactly. S2 makes movement and structure physical: travel follows cost of transport (M^0.68), lifting is work (M), sustained effort Kleiber (M^0.75), flapping flight M^(7/6); tissue stress grows as s, so falls, hard landings, and wing and limb loading bound larger bodies, while jumps keep the anatomy's own height and size grants no speed. See [S1 body size](MORPHOLOGY-S1-BODY-SIZE-2026-10-28.md) and [S2 physics](MORPHOLOGY-S2-PHYSICS-2026-10-28.md).
