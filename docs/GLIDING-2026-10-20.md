# Finite-height gliding and canopy access

This is a connected development pass, not full-game or creator acceptance.

## Implementation

`src/lineage/gliding.js` owns `finite-height-gliding/1`, bilateral surface checks, launch eligibility, continuous forward motion, bounded turning, descent, movement energy and landing damage. Launch needs a real downhill drop ahead or a supported living canopy. Gliders cannot hover or climb in the air. Rising terrain, exhausted support, boundaries and unsupported water landings cause a real surface landing; no teleport or resource rescue is provided. Folding high above the ground causes injury.

The shared simulation calls the same physics for controlled and autonomous organisms. Wildlife can launch while fleeing downhill; autonomous nesting/care can use costed tree climbing. Powered flight does not charge wing-flapping metabolism to membrane-only gliders. Ordinary metabolism and existing aerial interaction restrictions still apply. Regional page residency records movement; tracks and movement history use the existing authority.

The new non-exclusive patagium uses climber-leg precursors, normal mutation, shared developmental symmetry and parental recombination. It adds mass/upkeep and land movement cost. Existing relic membranes also support gliding without changing their historical surface modifiers. Two actual climbing legs allow a funded move into a mature Woodspire whose finite stand supports the body. This is a discrete tree-climb action, not continuous climbing physics or arbitrary vertical traversal.

Mouse actions expose Climb and Glide, with keyboard alternatives 7 and 0. Glide continuously advances without input; steering changes heading at a bounded rate. The action displays remaining relative height and warns about folding. Native 32px flank membranes deploy from their real sockets, retain eight facings, and hold a spread pose instead of a walking/flapping gait. The aerial shadow offset follows actual relative height. Generic action-result audio remains connected; no dedicated glide/landing sound or listening acceptance is claimed.

New worlds save the policy. Midair altitude, heading and mode persist; unknown policies and corrupt glide state fail restoration. Regional archives must agree on the policy and preserve valid aerial state. Historical absent-policy worlds keep their old movement and mutation choices, including absence of new patagium mutation options. No historical migration is performed. Consequently the bundled historical Day 30/100 worlds currently require a future explicit backed-up continuation adoption flow before they can use gliding; authored membranes alone cannot bypass that boundary.
