Survive. Evolve. Leave a Legacy.

GenVo is a top-down evolutionary survival sandbox set on a living planet. Every creature's anatomy decides how it moves, senses, feeds, survives and reproduces. Across generations, inherited changes and the pressures of the world shape the organisms that live in it, including yours.

You play one creature at a time. Find food and water, avoid predators, court a mate and raise young. When one of your descendants is grown, you carry on as them.

What you'll find

  • Bodies that matter. Parts decide what you can do: webbed limbs swim, digging forelimbs burrow, spring legs leap, wings glide, wide eyes see farther, claws and jaws change how you fight and feed.
  • Families and inheritance. Offspring inherit a physical mixture of both parents. When a pregnancy begins you can shape the coming generation in the Creature Lab, or let inheritance alone decide.
  • A world that evolves without you. Wild populations feed, hunt, breed, compete and change on their own. Lineages rise, spread and disappear.
  • A living planet. Procedural regions with rivers, lakes, woodland and open ground, seasons, weather, ice and wildfire, plants that grow, seed and spread, and an ecosystem that keeps running.
  • The First Legacy. Establish your life, raise a family, then continue your lineage as a grown descendant. Eight generations is a longer milestone. After either, the world stays fully playable.

Nothing here is scripted: there is no predefined final creature and no guaranteed mutation. What your lineage becomes depends on what actually happens.

How to start

Click Run game, choose New World, pick a Quick start creature (jumper, burrower, flier or shore swimmer) and create the world. Follow Your first goals: eat and drink, find a mate, make a home. The goal pill at the top then tracks Legacy 1/3 to 3/3; press H when a grown descendant is ready.

You can also start in a real evolved valley (Day 30 or Day 100), take over a living resident, or design a founder. The game autosaves in your browser; use Continue to pick up later on the same browser and device.

Controls

WASD / Arrows Move · Shift Sprint
E Interact / eat · Shift+E Drink · Q Next nearby food
Space Attack · Hold J Brace
Mouse Click a creature, plant or water for its actions · right-click ground to move there
H Continue as a grown descendant · Tab Switch family member · L Family journal
Y Field guide · ? Help · P Pause · Esc Menu
The Auto button in the in-game Menu lets your creature act on its own; move to take control back.

Status

GenVo is in active development and this is a jam build. Expect rough edges, and long sessions can be demanding on slower machines. A lighter performance mode switches on by itself when frames run slow, and fullscreen is under Esc → Options.

View: Esc → Options → View offers Comfort View (default: the land stays readable while creatures and food appear only when your creature senses them), Biological Vision (only what your creature's eyes and senses reach) and Sandbox View (the whole landscape and its plants, for enjoying the world).
