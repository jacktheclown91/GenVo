# First generation embodied heir stakes | October 21, 2026

## Why

The player should experience one embodied creature, its independently living descendants, and consequences of inherited form. A menu-only family stat or invented tutorial quest is not enough. This first coherent pass makes an **actual G2 descendant** understandable before and during a real handoff.

## Real authorities and contracts

`src/ui/lineage-stakes.js` is read-only presentation of the already canonical jumping, burrowing, climbing, gliding and diving anatomy rules. Only full functional mounted thresholds in worlds with corresponding physical rules produce ability labels. A descendant who has two valid spring legs may **grow up able to jump**, but it still needs real energy and safe launch conditions. Capability labels do not skip evolutionary precursors, edit a living body or promise that the environment offers the action at that moment.

The first-generation tutorial now identifies a **living successor**, optionally names its new or lost physical movement abilities, and preserves the existing `H` selection/handoff. Young that have not grown up remain under the family-care, learning and independent-practice logic. The generation-transition reveal surfaces the inherited body-verb difference alongside real portraits and historical pressure records.

The world-space guide marker is only offered for a **currently living, biologically visible descendant**, within the local camera-scale range and same stratum. A distant hidden heir remains a genealogy fact, not a perfect target-coordinate reveal. Predator alerts, illness, hunger, exposure and urgent young remain higher priority than celebratory progression. No additional save fields, simulation RNG draws, biological events or update frequency were introduced.

## Acceptance and unfinished work

`node tests/first-generation-heir-stakes.mjs` uses a real simulation, registers a descendant through canonical lineage/family controllers, gives it a valid test body with paired spring legs, verifies saved policy-dependent movement, and checks the actual objective branch and transition. The test **does not** claim naturally observed successful inheritance frequency, a completed 20–40-minute player session, improved fps, authored visual approval, or fully curated accessible four-verb starting scenarios.

Next: offer *truthful* first-generation anatomical action opportunities in prepared real-world starts (without forced mutations), then verify real heir progression and coherent first-time player interpretation in Chromium. Preserve the single canonical source and other draft PRs until reviewed.
