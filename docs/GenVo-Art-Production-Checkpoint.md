# GenVo — Imagegen Artwork Production Checkpoint

2026-10-14. Limited integrated pilot, not creator visual approval or completion of the broader development goal.

## Pipeline assessment and verified tools

The dedicated built-in image_gen tool actually generated eleven candidates/refinements in this session. No API key, additional paid service, Meshy or PixelLab was used. Pillow 12.3.0 is locally available. Node, the canonical indexed codec, Creature Lab source editor and bundled Chromium/Playwright remain the preparation, validation and runtime tools. No Aseprite, LibreSprite, GIMP or ImageMagick command was found on PATH; that does not prove they are absent elsewhere.

The previous anatomy scripts use ImageDraw polygons/ellipses, clipped shading and mathematically constructed contours. Flora uses repeated canvas pixel rectangles and lines. Increasing these counts cannot establish artistic quality. The current ground, shore and many bodies remain visibly repetitive or geometric. Existing runtime articulation and authoritative anatomy do not compensate for weak paintings.

## Art direction and resolution

Keep native 32×32 first. Matte living tissue, restrained cool shadows and warm sage highlights, readable asymmetry, minimal interior outlines, protected oral/eye materials and a shared inherited skin ramp. Ground cover should be low organic mats; shrubs need visible irregular branches and attached fruit. Individual anatomy remains interchangeable through the existing mounts and genotype. Never install whole-organism illustrations or species-specific cosmetic overrides.

A: Refined32 is promising for eyes, mouths and low plants. Current native reductions are explicit preparation of larger generated masters, not a claim the model delivered exact32 grids. B: No genuinely authored64 directional anatomy has been established, so no64 change is justified yet. Higher-resolution generated illustrations are not evidence of a superior native64 game. C: The pilot environment uses32 source rasters at the existing half-art-pixel scale, with nearest filtering; no global hybrid-resolution switch or blurry filtering. A future64 experiment must compare a genuinely authored equivalent part at the same world footprint and pixel density.

## Installed and rejected artwork

Installed: simple-eye open/blink and grazing-mouth relaxed/open, using creator AmphibianEye1.gif and GrazerMouth1.gif as meaningful design references. Two plant classes: sunmoss mat and bramble shrub with separate foliage-only/fruiting generated states. Originals remain unmodified. Masters, exact prompts and mechanical preparation script are preserved in assets/art/production-review. Authorship is AI-assisted generation plus mechanical preparation, not human drawing.

Rejected: both eight-view head sheets (incorrect/ambiguous directions, soft noise, invented marks or halo); torso candidate (does not convincingly improve the geometric mound); walking-leg (at native scale the shin loses volume and fails established coverage checks). Existing limb pixels were restored exactly. Installer rejects walking-leg. Do not use these candidates merely because generation succeeded.

Eye: stronger dark-center/light-rim identity and a readable eyelid; actual normal-scale gain is small. Mouth: stronger oral cavity and protected blunt pad; initial fang-like teeth were rejected and refined. It still risks reading as exaggerated lips, and rotated facings are not independently illustrated views. Moss: richer interconnected contour and shaded leaf masses; repeated use of one mat remains conspicuous. Shrub: better individual branching and attached fruit; not extensively represented in the selected shoreline, and one silhouette repeats. None is declared professional-quality or creator-approved.
