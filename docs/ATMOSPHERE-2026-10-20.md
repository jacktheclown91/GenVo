# Weather visibility and shared visual observation

New worlds record `atmospheric-visibility/1`. `src/ecology/atmospheric-visibility.js` derives fog, liquid precipitation, snow and optical extinction from existing regional rainfall/fronts, canonical local temperature, surface moisture and planetary wind. The snow fraction is the physical snowpack function. Fog uses a cool/wet-ground condensation proxy dispersed by wind; it is not a new atmospheric water inventory or a claimed complete humidity/dew-point simulation. Observation consumes no RNG, food, water or simulation time.

Visual transmission integrates extinction along the observer-target segment, with at most eight spatial samples. Existing biological sensing ranges remain inputs. Staged threat perception, background prey discovery/continued tracking, player-facing predator sight, place discovery and field-guide observations now use that shared attenuation. Vigilance does not bypass weather. Existing nearby hearing remains available in the staged threat system; danger music uses the same visual/hearing test rather than revealing an unseen distant hunter. This pass does not invent hearing organs, rain-noise masking or scent behavior. Learned places and food memories are retained.

Rendered creatures and direct picking use the shared visual-survey query, with inherited senses and mounted sensory factors. Lookout marks and food/water/threat cues also respect shared visibility; a hidden actor has no rendered screen position to click. Existing source observation radii remain explicit context inputs. The new presentation uses mist veils and moving wisps, wind-driven rain and snow, and a short reduced-sight cue. Weather remains visible in low-detail mode. Underground and submerged observers are not assigned surface fog; underground darkness and underwater optics remain separate unfinished work. The top-down camera still displays terrain; this is not a complete explored-terrain/fog-of-war model.

Historical saves retain their recorded visual behavior and legacy weather overlay unless they explicitly opt into current continuation rules. The existing original-backup/continuation transaction now includes visibility metadata and a separate source/tick/backup receipt, agrees across visited regions and rolls back all policy changes on failure. Existing organism bodies, ancestry, climate history, RNG and resource stocks are unchanged. No historical checkpoint or receipt was rewritten.

`tests/atmospheric-visibility.mjs` covers shared snow phase, optical loss, hearing, vigilance, sense advantage, underground exclusion, pre-climate temperature, read-only resource/RNG behavior, exact saved observations, historical absence and unknown-rule refusal. It also verifies that unseen distant hunters do not trigger danger music and nearby audible hunters still do. `tests/movement-adoption.mjs` covers full-state preservation, native-style transaction failure/rollback and real regional adoption/travel. All 27 focused checks, targeted threat-perception/predator-encounter, audio and audio-runtime checks pass; the unchanged weather/soil resume test closes its 14-day water budget. The audio escalation fixture now faces its actual hunter so it proves a perceptible threat rather than omniscient presence. Public-export parity and canonical-source audit pass.

`tools/verify-atmosphere-browser.mjs` runs normal New World and IndexedDB Continue against the exported browser build. It waits for the real first climate step, samples existing generated locations and stages only the player's observation position. No weather, terrain, body, age, reserves or ecological history is edited. The initial `ATMOSPHERE-BROWSER` world was too weak for the intended contrast; observed `CAVES-GEOLOGY` weather provides a wet/misty and clearer location without changing climate coefficients. Its read-only sight probe measures partial attenuation, not an observed predator pursuit. [Receipt and inspected screenshots](atmosphere-review-2026-10-20/receipt.json) preserve the actual samples and restored observation. This is automated browser observation, not creator visual acceptance.
