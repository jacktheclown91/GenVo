# 30-second days: clock infrastructure and first ecological findings (October 23, 2026)

**Creator decision:** a day lasts 30 seconds at 1x. Timing changes must not alter resource consumption, physiology, reproduction, ageing or ecology by accident.

This is the first stage of the survival rebalance (`design/GENVO-V1-PLAN-2026-10-23.md`, Phase 1). Metabolism, food properties and seasons are later stages, measured separately.

## What was built

`src/sim/day-clock.js`, policy `day-clock/30s`, saved with each world.

- **One tick rate for every world.** The simulation runs 8 ticks a second in both clocks. A day is 240 ticks on the 30-second clock and stays 48 on recorded worlds. Movement, perception, decisions, combat, diving, burrowing and every other real-time process are untouched.
- **A biological stride for daily biology.** Metabolism (resting costs, temperature, healing, starvation and thirst damage, aquatic feeding, gill water) and the old-age roll run on `CFG.bioStride`, which always falls 8 times a day. So their per-day totals are unchanged. Deaths from damage are still checked on the fast stride.
- **Decision-time biology is scaled.** Breeding drive, which rises and decays on each decision, is multiplied by `bioFactor()` (1 on recorded worlds, 0.2 on 30-second days).
- **Day-based values read live.** Five values were computed from `CFG.dayTicks` once at import: illness regrouping, weather aftermath, resource founding, offscreen-biology entry and regional debt. They now follow the active world's clock.
- **Each world applies its own clock** at construction, on restore and at the start of every tick. Background regions being prepared, or the menu's backdrop world, can therefore never change the active world's day. Region crossings copy the policy.
- **Ambient routines on the new clock.** On 30-second days, the ambient routines (browse, hop, roam, patrol) count their cycle on the biological stride and walk to a target anchored at the start of each window, then stay there. They no longer keep a target moving ahead of them.
- **Recorded worlds run exactly as before.** That includes saves and the evolved Day 31 and Day 101 checkpoints. For them `bioStride` equals `lifeStride` and `bioFactor()` is exactly 1.

**Opt-in for now:** new worlds get 30-second days only with `?day=30` in the address, until the ecology under the longer day is balanced and measured (below).

## Evidence

## What the longer day changes in the ecology

- **Wild animals walk more.** Their routines run in real time, so on the 30-second clock they walked 41 tiles a day instead of 8.4. Most of the increase came from ambient wandering (herd browsing and hopping, band wandering, roaming, prowling), which is the "meaningless wandering" in the brief.
- **Anchoring helps only partly.** It stops ambient walkers at their targets (traced) and brought the total to 36.5 tiles a day. The rest is real-time need and social movement: feeding trips, alarm flights, herd cohesion and spacing. These now happen many more times per day.
- **The result is hungrier animals:** a traced herd animal's energy sat near 0.5 instead of about 0.72. That means fewer births and, through hungrier predators, more predation.

This is a real consequence of giving every organism five times more waking real time per day under the same physiology. It is not yet a balanced ecosystem. The next stage has to decide, from biology, how much of that time animals spend active versus resting, before the metabolism stage changes daily needs.

## Rest when nothing is pressing (creator decision)

`src/ecology/ambient-allowance.js`, active on 30-second days only.

- **The rule:** an animal with no pressing need (food, water, safety, a mate, young) mostly rests, watches or browses in place.
- **The allowance:** ambient travel (wandering, roaming, patrolling, exploring) is limited to what its species walked without a need per day on the recorded clock. These were measured on three seeds over 8 days:

  | Species | Tiles per day |
  |---|---|
  | Stalker | 5.5 |
  | GenVo | 5.1 |
  | Podshell | 4.8 |
  | Mosskip | 4.1 |
  | Rillsifter | 2.2 |
  | Bankjaw | 2.0 |
  | Mirelick | 1.6 |
  | Rootling | 1.2 |

- **When it is spent:** rhythm routines keep their activity in place, and the fallback of exploring becomes rest.
- **Not limited:** needs are decided earlier and never limited. Every non-manual animal follows the rule, including the player's creature on AUTO.

**Wildlife travel per animal-day** (`tools/probe-need-movement.mjs`):

| | Recorded 6 s | 30 s before | 30 s after |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total | 8.4 | 36.5 | 18.4 |
| Without a need | 4.1 | 25.2 | 8.6 |
| With a need | 4.3 | 11.3 | 9.8 |

What remains is real-time reaction movement:
- alarm flights: 3.95 tiles against 1.59;
- feeding trips: 2.78 against 1.59;
- water and migration trips;
- herd cohesion and spacing: 2.5 against 0.7.

These are need-driven, or safety, so they are kept.

**360 days, same seed, player on AUTO** ([6 s](day-clock-2026-10-23/population-360d-6s.json), [30 s](day-clock-2026-10-23/population-360d-30s-rest-when-sated.json)):

| Day 361 | 6-second day | 30-second day |
|---|---|---|
| All animals | 983 | 628 |
| GenVo | 718 | 478 |
| Mosskip | 151 | 58 |
| Rootling | 32 | 54 |
| Stalker | 11 | 4 |
| Mirelick | 28 | 6 |
| Podshell | 43 | 27 |
| Bankjaw / rillsifter | 0 / 0 | 1 / 0 |
| Births | 1,118 | 701 |
| Predation / starvation / age deaths | 132 / 21 / 89 | 94 / 20 / 73 |

- **Same shape on both clocks:** a GenVo boom, aquatic species fading, and equally rare starvation.
- **The 30-second world is leaner,** with fewer mosskip, mirelick and Stalkers. Predation there is lower, so the gap comes from energy and births: the extra need-driven movement above.
- **Both baselines run away:** GenVo grows 20–30× in a year because daily needs are tiny. The metabolism stage redefines those limits, so the ecology is recalibrated after it rather than tuned now.

## Next steps

1. **Activity on the longer day:** measure and fix where wildlife moves without a need (cohesion re-targeting, spacing, explore). Animals with no pressing need should rest, watch or browse in place. Then compare 360-day populations between the two clocks.
2. **Metabolism stage:**
   - a resting adult should starve in 14–21 days and dehydrate in 3–5 days, faster with exertion, heat, cold, injury and reproduction;
   - penalties should start before death: reduced endurance, slower recovery, reproductive limits;
   - shared by every organism, and measured on matched seeds.
3. **Food-property stage:** each food's bite size and handling, and water from juicy food, measured on its own.
4. **Seasons and winter:** on matched seeds, compare passive, prepared and reckless strategies.
5. **Then:** make 30-second days the default for new worlds.
