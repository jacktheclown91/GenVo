# Ground vibration sensing | October 21, 2026

Evidence comes exclusively from recent actual `movedTick` physical steps, active energy-funded `burrowWork`, or saved `jumpLanding` impulses. Both organisms must be grounded and share a land mass or connected cave cells with compatible floors. Wet soil and atmospheric rain attenuate transmission; bristle and quill count and inherited senses determine range. The canonical part registry and upkeep already charge anatomical costs. No new geological inventory, RNG stream, or competing perception engine was added.

New worlds save `physical-ground-vibrations/1`; older saved worlds lacking the policy retain their behavior after ordinary Continue. Visited regional policies must agree. An explicit backup-based historical opt-in is deferred. This first pass has no frequency-specific materials, stone resonance, cross-depth travel, or quantified player field acceptance.
