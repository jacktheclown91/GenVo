# Anatomy-aware thermal refuge decisions

New worlds record `physiological-thermal-refuge/1`. Independent surface adults, including the selected creature in AUTO mode, can seek reachable nearby ground with lower thermal demand. Threats, torpor, family weather sheltering and family illness care retain their earlier priority. Manual control, urgent food/water reserves, dependent young and adults caring for dependent offspring are excluded. This is local thermal refuge, not a replacement for migration, brood transport or family shelter behavior.

`src/ecology/thermal-demand.js` now owns the composition formerly inline in normal metabolism: inherited comfort, proto-thermal factors, physical covering and wetness/density, mammalian heating, torpor and young/maternal protection. Metabolism uses that same result and pays it once from existing energy. `thermalSiteDemand` reads current temperature, moisture, coat state and usable family shelter at a hypothetical position without advancing physiology, consuming RNG or creating a reserve. A site comparison holds the current coat/body state fixed; it is not a long-term forecast of future coat development or reserves. Existing player-only weather exposure remains a separate pre-existing controller.

The bounded search considers at most 49 nearby grid positions and checks reachability for at most three ranked candidates. Inherited sensing bounds search distance. Improvement must exceed a travel penalty and minimum relief; there are no population persistence targets or named-species rescue rules. The ordinary route, locomotion and energy systems move the actor. Arrival changes actual exposure to the real local field; the behavior grants no healing, food or water. Resting on a refuge remains subject to urgent needs and the next normal decision. No tree cooling, underground access or shelter benefit is invented where the physical model does not supply it.

Native intent feedback identifies seeking/resting on milder ground. The existing mouse utility AUTO control invokes ordinary autonomous decisions; manual input retains authority. Targets and intents persist through standard actor saves. Historical saves keep recorded decisions unless explicitly adopted in the current-rules continuation. Optional policy/provenance agrees across visited regional archives and rolls back with the original-backed continuation transaction. Existing organism state, bodies, stock, ancestry and RNG are preserved by adoption.

## Evidence and limits

`tests/thermal-refuge.mjs` verifies read-only query state, body-dependent comfort, manual/urgent hydration priorities, normal autonomous decision, paid physical movement, actual metabolic relief and exact saved target. Its temperature field is an explicitly authored fixture. `tests/movement-adoption.mjs` retains whole-state, backup, atomic failure/rollback and actual regional travel checks for the new policy. All 29 focused playability checks pass, along with nursing conservation, egg/nest ecology and player weather-exposure checks.

`tools/verify-thermal-refuge-browser.mjs` uses normal New World, mouse Menu/AUTO and native IndexedDB Save/Continue. It stages the initial observation position and an explicitly recorded bounded temperature gradient on generated traversable ground. It edits no body, genes, reserves, population or weather clock. [Receipt and inspected frames](thermal-refuge-review-2026-10-20/receipt.json) show real charged walking into the milder cell, lower shared demand and restored intent/target. This is controlled gameplay integration, not proof of naturally emerging thermal gradients, ecological success or creator acceptance.

Still incomplete: natural refuge accessibility and lifetime choice quality, brood/caregiver relocation, physical tree-shade cooling, cave thermal-route planning, rain/wind refuges for all anatomies, complete model coupling, broader ecological testing and final art/audio. No tests or budgets were weakened, and no population outcome was tuned. The full Creature-stage game remains unfinished.
