# Ordinary dirty-region saves — October 21, 2026

Manual and scheduled saves now automatically use the existing atomic changed-page path after a complete same-world baseline. The active record is always captured. Unchanged archived and surveyed owners retain their exact indexed records; changed owners publish with the active state, metadata and index in one guarded transaction. This connects changed-page storage to ordinary gameplay SaveManager operation rather than leaving it as a standalone transport API.

The journal records the owner generations and shallow reference/value guards captured before synchronous IndexedDB cloning. Real biological replay, legacy environmental replay, boundary-ownership publication and in-place daily seam publication advance a private per-owner generation. Snapshot replacement and guarded core/world fields also detect migration histories, resident/founder provenance and changed owner descriptors. The canonical writer of any future in-place inactive-owner mutation must call `markRegionalOwnerDirty`; reference guards do not claim to discover arbitrary unannounced edits inside arrays. Unvisited survey Worlds remain unstepped under their existing authority and use the same explicit dirty-owner contract for future writers.

A successful save acknowledges the original capture ticket, never the newer live state when transport completes. A replay or physical publication after capture therefore remains dirty for the next save. Failed saves acknowledge nothing. New simulations, slot changes, Continue/install resets, clear, changed region ownership/order and external checkpoint replacements require a complete baseline. Clearing increments the journal epoch so a late completion cannot resurrect an old world's baseline. Guards use weak references, preventing the journal from keeping replaced archive payloads alive. Browsers without WeakRef retain complete saves rather than failing storage.
