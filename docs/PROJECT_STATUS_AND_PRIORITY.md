## Current checkpoint — dual-mode reproductive anatomy (development only)

Mounted `live-womb` + `egg-chamber` allow a single gestating creature to select either existing funded live birth or existing finite egg laying at conception. The optional combination carries high mutation costs, two flank slots, real ongoing metabolism and conception expenses. Player gestating dual creatures may cycle adaptive/live/egg mode with V in the mate panel. See `docs/DUAL_MODE_REPRODUCTIVE_EVOLUTION.md`. No full ecology balance or stable release acceptance is implied.

## Current checkpoint — finite egg ecology (development only)

Egg laying can create a paid nest from real local vegetation when affordable, and eggs may be tended or eaten by appropriate wild hunters. `brood-pouch` increases nest-care efficacy but does not enable egg laying. Explicit resource accounting and non-guaranteed survival replace any fixed brood success target. Reproductive water investment is capped at 30% of the laying parent's hydration. See `docs/EGG_NEST_CARE_AND_PREDATION.md`. Focused Node tests are required; endgame balance and visual runtime acceptance are not claimed.

## Modular reproduction first slice (development only)

Implemented: inherited `egg-chamber` body part, conception-frozen egg vs legacy live mode, physical incubating eggs, environmental loss, real hatchlings, save/load and UI counts. Now also implemented: finite nests, energy-costing parent care, brood-pouch-enhanced protection, egg predation by hungry wild hunters. Not implemented: anatomically gated live-birth organ, dual-mode regulator, egg transport, authored egg artwork. Continue **feature expansion with focused correctness tests**; do not use fixed seed survival/mating outcomes as mandatory success. See `docs/MODULAR_REPRODUCTIVE_ANATOMY.md`.

## Development sequence — expansion over microbalancing

1. Lock source authorities, rule-oriented tests, provenance and report index; preserve historic receipts.
2. Extend the implemented heritable preferences with comparative mate selection, display behavior and competition as a playable biological vertical slice. Keep transitory age, illness, hunger, energy and water as current state—not hereditary mate-preference genes.
3. Expand living interactions and ecological niches: new organisms/resources, actual diet and habitat functions, body part capabilities, movement styles, defenses and predator strategies.
4. Integrate Living Planet in staged terrain/vegetation/water/biological feedback passes, avoiding scripted outcome control.
5. Develop generation-to-generation player decisions, readable progression, full menus and independent save slots concurrently with simulation expansion.
6. At integration/release milestones, measure cross-seed variation and causal integrity, then balance for compelling *choices*, difficulty and stability rather than equal endgames.
