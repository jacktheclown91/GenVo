# Conception stages and inherited ecological support

## Diagnosis and substantive changes

The longstanding evolving-courtship conception assertion failed because its isolated performer had no mouth. The canonical diet resolver gave it no usable food, so local carrying pressure correctly prohibited breeding. Readiness reserves and a successful display do not manufacture ecological support. The failure occurred before reciprocal choice; it was neither hidden mate rejection nor inherited incompatibility.

`src/reproduction/conception-availability.js` now owns the existing final conception gates as an explainable assessment: physiological maturity/roles, approved inherited compatibility, current encounter/readiness, engineering-partner response, pregnancy/recovery, kinship and autonomous carrying support. Existing thresholds, manual-player ecological policy and fertility probabilities are retained. Display eligibility, reciprocal choice and final conception remain distinct stages.

`src/sim/simulation.js` calls that assessment, records an observable mechanical failure and ends the blocked suitor's stale courtship so survival behavior can resume. Autonomous partner search now checks candidate carrying support before choosing and funding a display; the final stage still rechecks on arrival. This prevents repeatedly selecting ecologically unsupported partners without forcing anyone to accept. It does not penalize preference learning or consume conception RNG/funding on a hard gate. An unrelated partner's active encounter and unrelated player target remain intact.

`src/ecology/carrying.js` now uses actual inherited crop nutrition when surveying food and breeding support. It previously reused the founding resource constant after player/ambient feeding had already switched to inherited nutrition. Live anatomy, reach, crop tissue/handling and finite stocks remain authoritative; no resource grants or balance thresholds were added.

## Normalization contract and remaining limits

The creator approved preserving adult-prey units with anatomy-based normalization. The previously failing freshwater hunter-capacity test now passes. `src/ecology/prey-normalization.js` first uses the largest living, canonically eligible prey body converted to its adult unit through the existing developmental mass fraction, retaining the previous mixed-community calibration. With no such body, the explicit mounted part masses provide a conservative stable adult anatomy reference. Missing body data yields no reference; no species recipe fabricates one. Eligibility still comes from canonical feeding/prey/access rules, and the reference contributes no food or automatic breeding permission.

Accessible sampled aquatic energy is still divided by reference mass times the established detritus-nutrition unit. Existing prey counts, minimum prey needs, physiological reserves, crowding thresholds and resource thresholds remain unchanged. Mounted part mass is used only for this missing-reference normalization; it does not replace canonical physical body mass, metabolism or movement costs. This is a provisional unit contract, not a wholesale energy-budget recalibration. A future energy-budget model must replace this conversion deliberately rather than add another authority.

Tests cover aquatic-only food, real mixed communities, inaccessible water, depleted supply, species-label-independent fallback and missing explicit anatomy. The native browser uses existing natural aquatic stocks with valid mounted aquatic/hunting anatomy and no terrestrial actors: actual feeding depletes a real fry stock, while the finite accessible community contributes to the unchanged carrying calculation. No food or terrain stock was authored into that native scene.

A separate fresh-world run through 4,800 ordinary full-lifecycle updates (600 simulation seconds, accelerated wall clock) recorded 66 animal births, three botanical cross-fertilizations and endpoint save parity. There were no position, anatomy, reserve, terrain or genotype fixtures and no player. This is natural simulation evidence, not a rendered human ten-minute experience or adaptive equilibrium proof.

Population/crowding authority still contains founding-label distinctions; maturity/gestation and metabolic defaults retain existing physiological catalog context. This pass does not claim all phenotype authority is complete. It also does not establish long-run adaptive balance, subjective audio/visual approval, human first-time playtesting, natural generation transitions or terrain height/support.
