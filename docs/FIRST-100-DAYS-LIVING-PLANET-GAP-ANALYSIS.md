## Ordered implementation plan

The four passes below retain the full requested scope. Each item uses one canonical authority and small mechanism checks. Checkpoints are integration gates, not an excuse to run all worlds after every change. October 30 is the feature-freeze target, not permission to silently omit a required mechanism.

### Pass 1: complete physical environmental foundations

1. **Regional field ownership and generation.** Extend the existing World/terrain model with persistent elevation-derived temperature/rainfall, water depth and salinity; derive connected terrain gradients and freshwater-estuary-coast-ocean topology. Keep the ordinary New World path as the production initializer, not a test-only habitat map. Include temperate, wetland, dryland, cold and mountain conditions with traversable connections and appropriate barriers. Persist/version physical state and make field diagnostics readable. Initially cold/saline regions are candidate environments, not claims of functional organisms.
2. **Meaningful mountain traversal and hydrology.** Read slope/grade in the shared movement profile and route cost/reach logic, with body-supported limits and real energy use. Preserve existing conservative groundwater and explicitly account for routed surface water if introduced. Give terrain enough visual relief to explain effort and drainage. Focused checks: uphill/downhill effort, route alternatives/barriers, manual/autonomous parity, water closure and save determinism.
3. **Freshwater and marine consequences.** Make potable water, shore food, aquatic primary production and resource demand consult actual salinity/depth. Account for salt/water transfers and bounded external reservoirs; no freshwater-free-hydration shortcut at sea. Reuse existing finite aquatic resource stores with explicit marine producer behavior, not reskinned freshwater labels. Link to Pass 2 osmoregulation before claiming marine viability.
4. **Producer breadth and competition.** Add the smallest functional woody/canopy producer and relevant cold/dry/saline producer responses using existing flora genomes, growth funding, establishment and dispersal. Canopy changes local light/cover and consumes biomass/resources. Temperature and salt tolerance are inherited where required; local suitability gates germination/growth. Compare controlled contrasts, not preferred population totals. Review documented forgiving reed moisture response separately; do not retune it merely to preserve grazers.

Environmental pass gate: all physical/resource fields are integrated and deterministic, directed flow and competition have focused proof, and the connected world renders honestly. Full cold/marine survival acceptance waits for Pass 2 rather than an artificial survivor population.

### Pass 2: complete biological foundations

5. **Remove founder-relative rescue dependencies.** Rework active reproduction-drive/search and kin-relaxation decisions around encounter evidence, actual reproductive condition and available kin. Ensure extinction remains possible and no off-map organisms/resources appear. Preserve kinship, inbreeding, finite birth-budget and functional predation assertions. Do not disable meaningful failing tests to hide changed contracts.
6. **Independent pigment inheritance.** Add dedicated pigment traits through the existing animal creation/inheritance path, per-region symmetric expression and indexed palette rendering. Derive old looks once for legacy creatures; new variation comes through parental recombination/mutation. Validate both parents, hybrids, asymmetry route, no mutation of living parents, legacy appearance and bounded tint behavior. This remains appearance-only; do not combine it with camouflage or colour-preference balance.
7. **Shared heat balance and thermal behavior.** Replace, rather than layer on, the comfort-gap charge. Add saved body heat/wetness and inherited capacities; use mass/surface, metabolic work, local air/water, solar/wind/rain and shelter exchange. Generalize the player-only exposure authority into shared biological state. Let sensed heat/body condition cause basking, shade, shelter or reduced activity through existing decision/movement systems. Add the planned compact thermal indicator only after the state is real. Validate heat-flow signs, energy/hydration cost, cold/heat limits, player/wild parity and exact resume.
8. **Functional regional coverings and habitat adaptation.** Extend canonical anatomy/tissue properties with minimum insulating/evaporative/protective coverings and funded development, inherited through the existing mirrored plan. Introduce continuous osmotic tolerance/regulation and water-loss costs through the same physiology, with no species or biome immunity. Verify freshwater/saline/land transitions and compatible egg/birth habitats. Cold and marine starter organisms are normally initialized with explicit inherited capabilities, not label bonuses or post-collapse replacement animals.

Biological pass gate: one set of physical survival rules supports the representative habitats; coverings, pigment, anatomy and reproductive traits inherit and persist; selection acts through realized survival/reproduction, not pressure labels awarding outcomes. No guaranteed speciation or minimum survivor count.

### Pass 3: complete the player life cycle

9. **Mouse-first survival and exploration.** Audit actual movement/resource/context targets in each environment. Make current capabilities, food, water, hazards, cover and home actions legible; show why an action is unavailable. Preserve keyboard alternatives. Review interactions in the real rendered game after each gameplay change.
10. **Courtship to descendant control.** Exercise an ordinary initialized founder's approach, compatible courtship, mating, gestation/eggs, offspring care, maturation, succession and inherited/editor body changes. Include a biologically possible hybrid opportunity with transparent fertility consequences. Reuse existing reproduction, family, succession, editor and generation controllers. Fix broken links and unclear cues rather than duplicating systems. Life-history differences remain real; faster lineages demonstrate multiple generations only when timing permits.
11. **Understandable living history.** Explain local habitat/resource changes, heredity, dispersal, predation and deaths with actual receipts. Keep player agency in survival, mate choice, care, range and lineage decisions. Acceptance is a coherent playable journey, not an automated script awarding a generation.

Player pass gate: recorded real gameplay demonstrates the survival-to-evolution route with current production initialization, mouse-first interactions and inspected offspring/lineage differences. Controlled fixtures prove rare mechanisms separately; they cannot impersonate ordinary-world opportunities.

### Pass 4: integrate, polish, then run the milestone trial

12. **Performance and presentation integration.** Check input, camera, terrain readability, organism identification, anatomy/pigment/covering expression, interface obstruction and save/load in real gameplay. Take bounded CPU/render profiles only for demonstrated blocking cost. Investigate cohort/memory growth without discarding positive rare stock or destroying provenance to obtain green performance. Use existing indexed/batched renderer and cache bounds. Preserve known failures and report limits.
13. **Canonical Living Planet Trial.** After the mechanisms above integrate, run one normally initialized connected world through 100 completed days with baseline and days 1,10,30,60,100. Pair ecological observations with ordinary player gameplay in day bands 1-20,20-40,40-70,70-100. Capture events, opportunities, resource/heat/water/salt balances, inheritance, frame-time/input/memory evidence and interrupted/resumed parity. Explain each collapse; do not require equilibrium, universal survival, dramatic evolution or speciation. Then use a small number of contrasting seeds (initial plan: two, selected for actual environmental contrasts) to challenge the same rules, not five automatic centuries on every edit.
