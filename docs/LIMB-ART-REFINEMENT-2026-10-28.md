# Distinct articulated limb tissue at64/128

The prior optional walking, runner and sturdy legs reused a single generated tissue master at different transverse widths. The production candidate now has six native indexed assets with independently authored anatomical silhouettes and shading:

- Walking: distinct muscle belly, insertion folds, knee surface, shin and separated digital pads.
- Runner: slimmer proximal muscle and long narrow tendon/shin, with a longer central digital pad.
- Sturdy: broader muscle/joint/ankle and load-spreading digital pads.

Rest/contact and recovery tissue differ: the existing planted/lifted frame selection now flexes actual distal paint. Five-tone inherited skin shading includes broken fascicles and highlight stippling; this is authored at64/128, not a32px enlargement. These surfaces communicate existing leg anatomy and grant no new claws, armor, support capacity or biological effects.

It preserves exact registered upper/lower anchors, hip/knee insertion regions, root pivot, display scale, timing, palette regions and eight-facing contract. No runtime gait mechanism, simulation, inheritance, resource, save or global resolution setting changed. Native32, original masters and the other24 candidate variants remain intact.
