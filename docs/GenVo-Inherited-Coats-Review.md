# Inherited protective coats: integrated development checkpoint

Clonal offspring from lineages already owning finite coat tissue can inherit thicker or thinner coat allocation. Existing adults and old schema 1/2 genomes remain unchanged. Schema 3 inherits seed and coat together; seed/cargo/bank cohorts and new established adults retain both. One trait axis is chosen per attempted mutation, keeping the existing 12% packet mutation rate. Coat steps are 0.025 with provisional bounds 0.10–0.40; seed bounds and rates remain unchanged. These are provisional balance values, not an established optimum or sexual reproduction model.

Thicker coats partition more of the same biomass into discarded protective tissue, reduce edible flesh and raise the existing paid handling cost. Mounted crushing/pecking capabilities reduce effort through the same authoritative feeding path. No new feeding, anatomy or reproductive controller was introduced. Native pod shells reflect coat allocation in world art and Inspect. Small plants use one stem; actual nearby coat-bearing crops are visible beneath dominant vegetation rather than becoming invisible food. This is limited to the creature's local two-and-a-half-tile clarity pocket.

Runtime inspection also uncovered an interaction regression: pointer clicks inside the object inspector dismissed its explicit target, allowing E to fall back to nearby food. The tray now preserves selection inside its own inspector. The real capture verifies inspect → close → E feeds the exact selected parent genotype. E still performs an action directly, Q cycles, and the mouse retains the radial menu.

## Evidence and limits

Thirteen focused checks passed: the twelve in inherited-coats-tests.json plus canonical-source. Controlled verification covers two-generation inheritance, fixed adults, schema/range rejection, real germination, finite tissue accounting, specialized handling, save parity, E/Q and shared action states. Exact prior delivered source migration passed, including unchanged coat/genotype stocks (inherited-coats-prior-save-receipt.json).

Actual Chromium at 800×600 selected, inspected and directly ate genuinely generated thin (0.225) and thick (0.275) coat variants. Their actual bites cost 0.3313 and 0.6600 energy respectively; bite amounts differ, so the controlled equal-biomass comparison establishes the trait's causal effect. This capture accelerated 180 canonical funded vegetation phases and placed the player/camera; it did not assign plant genotypes or biomass. It is controlled runtime evidence. See inherited-coats-proof.json, screenshots and inherited-coats-runtime.webm. The separate E/Q browser workflow also passed again after the inspector fix.

A separate 30-second normal fresh world, without biological/terrain/reserve/position fixtures, recorded two coat genotypes in seed records, zero coat-variant adults and three animals with paid handling receipts at tick 239. This does not establish natural adult turnover, adaptive selection, balanced ecology, or human comprehension.

Three seeds each ran 100 resource-only days: tracked mineral-equivalent inventory drift stayed below 0.000005 units; coat variants established without injected traits. These exclude animal lifecycles and are conservation evidence, not ecological acceptance. Existing historical habitat-fixture and birth/courtship failures remain documented; no full-suite pass or regression-free claim is made.

The coat-allocation implementation milestone is complete. Next meaningful priority: longer ordinary plant–consumer gameplay and lineage observations, then anatomy-driven processing responses where they create new activities. Coat-dependent gut survival, pollen/sexual flora rules and aerial/vertical locomotion remain unimplemented design work. No creator visual approval, human playtest success or evolutionary arms race is claimed.
