# Condition-driven habitat soundscapes

Implemented four nonvocal texture layers—canopy rustle, dry-ground grit, exposed air and enclosed air—inside the existing WebAudio director. They share its one cached noise buffer, with filtered bands and slow bounded modulation. No new samples, fictional creature calls, weather events, ecology clocks or per-update audio-node allocation were added.

`src/audio/habitat.js` reads canonical `biomeAt`, wind, actual woodspire stock and passage origin. It differentiates woodland/grassland/wetland, alpine/tundra, arid/steppe, saline water, cave/burrow and submerged mixes. Rain softens dry-ground and canopy texture; real wind strength controls the new outdoor layers. Enclosed scenes suppress outdoor beds and use a low-pass acoustic filter; cave and burrow air have different bands. Existing music, anatomical voices, foreground ducking and the authored codec fallbacks remain.

Water-edge ambience now requires an actual nearby owned liquid cell, rather than seepage or stale distance data alone. Empty inventory and sealed ice stay quiet; exposed liquid through a real crack remains eligible. The nearest source sets a smoothly eased stereo pan, with mono-routing fallback where StereoPanner is unavailable. Salinity changes the water bed's pace and balance. No neighbouring region is realized by the query, and no water is created.

Runtime changes: `src/audio/habitat.js`, `habitat-textures.js`, `model.js`, `audio.js`. Mix transitions reuse the existing ambience/music buses and mute/master controls. The original 33 assets and their masters remain unchanged: 4,674,284 source bytes and 1,545,682 shipped bytes, within the unchanged asset-test budgets. The new texture graph has four shared-buffer sources and four slow modulators.
