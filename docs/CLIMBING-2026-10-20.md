# Continuous canopy climbing

New worlds record `continuous-canopy-climbing/1`. The existing Climb/Descend button and 7 now initiate continuous vertical travel on an actual supporting Woodspire instead of teleporting between surface and canopy. Two inherited climbing legs enable ground ascent; existing supported gripping anatomy permits descent. Body load sets speed and work. Full ascent still costs three times adult body mass; descent also costs work. Ordinary metabolism continues. Pressing Descend during ascent reverses toward ground without resetting height.

`src/lineage/climbing.js` owns the shared step, transition status, height contact and state validation. Wildlife nesting/care and threat escape use the same entry path and tick-driven traversal as the player. Horizontal movement stays anchored. Canopy food, nests, flight and gliding cannot be accessed halfway up a trunk. Native limbs cycle through a gripping pose above a height-dependent position and shadow, with a visible height reading, action feedback and existing confirmation/landing/damage cues. Low trunk height remains reachable from the surface; middle-trunk contact requires compatible heights. Canopy support loss, overload or failed grips causes damage proportional to actual fall height. Exhaustion reverses toward the ground.

Pregnancies remain funded but undelivered during a climb. Modern live births descend first; an egg layer unable to construct a canopy clutch descends before laying. Existing canopy feeding, care and finite nest-material authorities remain in charge. No tree stock, food, water or RNG is created by traversal.

Historical worlds retain their recorded instant traversal. The existing explicit backed-up movement continuation can opt into the new rule through `climbing-rules.js`, independently of prior jumping/gliding/diving receipts. SaveManager commits backup and continuation atomically and rolls back every new policy field on failure. Regional policy/history agreement, actual visited-region adoption/return and exact mid-trunk saves are verified.
