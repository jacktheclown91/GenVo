# E16: Physically Accompanied Family Crossings

## New player interaction
At a valid planetary frontier, the dock offers **Family: solo / Family: together**. This is an explicit player choice. Solo remains the default for compatibility with E15, but it refuses to abandon living dependent young. With family travel selected, actual adult relatives/mates within nearby real distance, plus their true nursing/dependent offspring, can cross as one cohort using the existing **Cross Region (9)** action. Cohorts are capped at eight physically present creatures. This is not a summoning ability: distant young must follow the family first. The UI displays party size and why a crossing is unavailable.

## Biological governance
- Family membership uses the existing family/mate/kinship controller, rather than generic species identity or labels.
- A dependent's living caregiver and lactation parent both must be in the traveling cohort. A baby cannot be dropped, kidnapped away from a live caregiver, or duplicated in the source archived population.
- All group members individually pass the real global geographic barrier. Non-swimmers cannot be ferried through a sea crossing by arbitrary teleport; no special exemptions are assigned by species.
- Sleeping, pregnant, actively fighting, courtship-occupied or exhausted adult escorts are not eligible.
- Each traveler retains its actual body parts/genome, inherited preferences, age, health, dependent relationships and individual ID, while paying its own mass-dependent journey energy cost.
- The canonical source-region archive records exact original indexes of multiple travelers and excludes their living instances. On return, the same creatures are reinserted at those positions, so wildlife iteration and population ownership are stable. Existing E15 single-traveler archived saves remain compatible.

## Still missing
These are **explicit local family expeditions**. It is not yet autonomous wild group migration or unattended background evolution. Inactive populations remain frozen. No cross-border automatic plant/seed/water flux, nonlocal mating, long-distance newborn rescue or streamable continuous camera movement is claimed. E17 should establish independently budgeted, observable remote weather/soil progression while retaining strict wildlife/controller lifecycles.
