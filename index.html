Basics and lineage

You control one GenVo at a time. Once another lineage member is living, Tab switches between family members. The automation button in the top menu lets the selected creature use its own survival AI. Move or interact to take manual control back immediately.

The loop: survive → pair → raise young → prepare a grown descendant → hand control forward. L opens the family journal. H transfers control when a mature descendant is eligible.

Generation 8 is a milestone, not an ending. The ecosystem keeps running after G8, and the lineage can continue.

Saving: GenVo autosaves in the browser and after major lineage events. A saved lineage can be continued after a refresh or later visit on this browser/device.

Autosave is on.
Survival and Stalkers

Move WASD / arrows · Sprint Shift · Interact E · Drink Shift+E · Attack Space · Brace hold J · Cycle nearby compatible food Q · Pause P.

Mouse: Click an object to select it and see its actions. Right-click ground to choose Move here. Interact uses your selected target; click food directly to choose it. With nothing selected, Q cycles compatible nearby food.

Breath: sprinting, bracing and attacking use breath. Walking and standing refill it quickly. When you are winded you cannot sprint or brace and attacks wind up slower, but you can still walk.

Stalker response: a Stalker chases faster than you walk but slower than you sprint, and it tires. Sidestep the marked charge lane, hold J facing a charge to blunt it, or bite during its recovery. Two clean wounds drive it off. Dense plants break its sight if you move quietly. When teaching young, escaping with them safely is a valid danger lesson.

Family and shelter

Speed the speed button or [ and ] · 2x and 4x hand your creature to AUTO. Moving or acting takes it back at 1x. Danger to your creature or family slows time back to 1x and a short summary says what happened.

Switch family member Tab · Mates M · Court C · Journal L · Hand off lineage H · Shelter B · Build / repair N · Store food F.

Family direction: 1 Auto · 2 Follow · 3 Forage · 4 Home · 5 Scout after the family sustains enough adults and familiar ground. Use the target icon or press R, then click or tap ground for local guidance.

New nests become available as the family grows. Relatives move to viable marked ground and build from local plant material.

Range, population, and species

Home range T · Population O · Migration K · Take route G.

Food is shared ecology. Family members, wildlife, and other living taxa can thin the same ground. Population centers have local carrying limits.

When multiple species coexist, guidance follows the active taxon by default. The command wheel can explicitly switch to the whole lineage.

Evolution and lifestyles

Repeated behavior and habitat use shape descendants. Forager and Colony forms, population pressure, proto-traits, species branches, and lifestyles emerge from play rather than a conventional skill tree.

Aquatic branches can make open water normal travel space. Nomadic, Guardian, and Burrower branches change movement, defense, shelter use, and settlement behavior in different ways.
